## Anthropic

### [NEW] "When AI Builds Itself": Claude authors 80%+ of Anthropic's codebase; conditional global pause proposed

Published June 4, Anthropic's paper reveals that as of May 2026 more than 80% of code merged into Anthropic's production codebase was authored by Claude — up from low single digits before February 2025. The typical Anthropic engineer now merges roughly 8× as much code per day as in 2024. The paper warns that "recursive self-improvement" — an AI autonomously designing its own successor — "could come sooner than most institutions are prepared for." Anthropic's central recommendation is that the world should have the *option* to pause frontier AI development "to enable societal structures and alignment research to keep up," calling a worldwide slowdown "likely a good thing" while explicitly not committing to a unilateral halt. [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-warns-ai-may-soon-begin-recursive-self-improvement/), [SOFX](https://www.sofx.com/anthropic-calls-for-global-ai-pause-as-claude-writes-its-own-code/), [full analysis](https://themenonlab.blog/blog/anthropic-recursive-self-improvement-ai-builds-itself-june-2026/)

**Why it matters:** The 80% figure is the first public disclosure that a major AI lab is already running on AI-generated code at this scale — it's a concrete data point on how quickly the "AI writes AI" loop is closing, relevant to anyone thinking about software economics or safety timelines.

### [NEW] Anthropic Economic Futures Research Fund: $200M for job-impact research, $150M national fellowship

On June 10 Anthropic announced the Economic Futures Research Fund ($200M to back research trials and program evaluation on public policies addressing AI-driven labor displacement) and a $150M national fellowship program targeting early-career professionals in communities most exposed to AI disruption. CEO Dario Amodei simultaneously published the essay "Policy on the AI Exponential" arguing the disruptions could be larger and longer-lasting than any prior technological shift, and proposing a tiered US government response framework keyed to unemployment milestones of 5%, 10%, and an "unprecedented" unspecified level. [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/business/articles/2026-06-10/anthropic-pledges-200-million-to-research-ais-economic-impact-as-ceo-suggests-job-loss-solutions), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jun/10/ceo-suggests-job-loss-solutions-anthropic-pledges-200-million/)

**Why it matters:** Anthropic is essentially pre-funding the policy research it wants Congress to cite. The tiered unemployment-trigger framework is a concrete model for how AI companies might engage with regulators before legislation catches up.

### [NEW] Meta Business Agent globally available on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger

Announced at Meta's Conversations conference in London on June 3, the Meta Business Agent is now globally available to businesses on WhatsApp Business, Instagram Pro, and Messenger. It handles customer questions, product recommendations, appointment booking, and sales lead qualification, and routes to a human when needed. More than 1M businesses were already using it through a two-year pilot in India, Mexico, and Brazil. ## Model Releases

### [NEW] Gemma 4 12B: encoder-free multimodal, 16GB VRAM, Apache 2.0

Google released Gemma 4 12B on June 3. The key architectural move: vision and audio inputs flow directly into the LLM backbone with no separate multimodal encoders — the first mid-sized Gemma with native audio input. It sits between the E4B and the 26B MoE in the Gemma family and requires just 16GB VRAM to run locally. Benchmarks near the 26B MoE at less than half the total memory footprint. Apache 2.0. Available on Hugging Face and via the Google AI Developer ecosystem. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemma-4-12b/), [Developer Guide](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemma-4-12b-the-developer-guide/)

**Why it matters:** Encoder-free multimodal architecture is the direction Apple's Foundation Models framework is also heading (per WWDC). A 16GB-VRAM open-weight model with audio input is immediately useful for local agent pipelines.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] New York legislature passes 7 AI bills; Governor Hochul has until Dec 31 to sign

New York lawmakers adjourned their 2026 session June 5 after passing seven AI-related bills, the most significant package from any US state legislature this cycle. Bills sent to Governor Hochul include: (1) **Kids Chatbot Safety Act** — prohibits AI companion chatbots for minors; (2) **AI Training Data Transparency Act** — requires generative AI developers to publish high-level dataset summaries; (3) **FAIR News Act** — mandates disclosure of AI-generated news content; (4) a **data center moratorium** limiting new construction; and (5) a ban on **AI-assisted surveillance pricing**. Hochul has until December 31, 2026 to sign or veto; she can also let bills become law without signature. [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/new-york-lawmakers-pass-kids-ai-chatbot-safety-bill-and-two-transparency-acts), [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=91697b73-ce5f-408d-8917-a8ba2ee390e0)
