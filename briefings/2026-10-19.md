## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Anthropic commits 5% of shares to new Public Benefit Fund ahead of IPO

**Background:** Anthropic has been on an IPO roadshow targeting a Thanksgiving-window Nasdaq listing at a $1.5T–$2T valuation (down from a $2T consensus after Q3 revenue of $16.8B came in below expectations).

**New:** Anthropic announced it will commit 5% of its shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund — a separate entity whose stake grows as the company's value grows. Stockholder approval required. The board may contribute an additional 2% for every 50% increase in Anthropic's valuation above a set baseline. The structure is designed to lock in Anthropic's public-benefit mission at a corporate level as it transitions from a private benefit corporation to a publicly traded company. Source: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-commits-5-of-its-shares-to-new-public-benefit-fund/).

**Why it matters:** The economic terms mean the Fund grows with Anthropic's market cap, creating a credible external beneficiary that constrains future boards from walking away from the mission.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] JPMorgan and Schwab block outside AI agents; Schwab builds MCP servers as the sanctioned path

JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon said on October 13 earnings that JPMorgan blocks external AI agents from customer accounts entirely, citing "rogue agent" risk. Agents can read balance data via Plaid connectors in read-only mode but cannot move money. Dimon warned that customers who hand agents account credentials accept a "liability transfer."

Charles Schwab CEO Rick Wurster confirmed Schwab blocks agents (specifically naming Meta's Muse) from using client login credentials or browser automation. Schwab's solution: it is building its own MCP servers — authenticated, bank-controlled connections that let a user's chosen AI model query Schwab data under rules Schwab sets, rather than scraping sessions. Schwab is also directing clients toward its own assistant, Charley.

Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/schwab-blocks-ai-agents-meta-muse), [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/schwab-blocks-unauthorized-ai-agents-points-clients-to-charley), [Fortune / Dimon](https://fortune.com/2026/10/15/jamie-dimon-ai-agents-meta-muse-jpmorgan/), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/jpmorgan-chase-blocks-outside-ai-agents/).

**Why it matters:** Schwab's MCP server build-out is the first major consumer financial institution publicly committing to MCP as its agent integration layer. The pattern — block rogue credential-scraping agents, offer a structured MCP handshake instead — is exactly what the spec was designed to enable and will likely become an industry template.

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] OpenAI contributes $5M to Simons Foundation for AI-generated math verification

OpenAI announced a $5 million contribution to a Simons Foundation initiative focused on verifying AI-generated mathematical research. The effort builds on OpenAI's August 2026 Astra math breakthrough (10 open problems solved with Lean 4 proofs) and addresses a gap: most AI math results still require human expert review to confirm soundness. Source: [AI weekly coverage](https://aiweekly.co/).

**Why it matters:** The verification bottleneck is the limiting factor for deploying AI math solvers in serious research. A funded Simons program targeting this directly is the right infrastructure investment.
