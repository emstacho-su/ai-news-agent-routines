## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Google I/O Eve: Gemini Spark full agent leak ahead of May 19 keynote
**Background:** Google's Android Show (May 12) previewed Gemini Intelligence for Android and confirmed the I/O keynote for May 19 at 10am PT. Gemini Omni (unified text/image/video) and an agentic cross-app AI were expected but the agent was previously unnamed in this briefing.

**New:** Multiple leaks from Android Authority and 9to5Google this week confirmed the agent is named **Gemini Spark**, with full onboarding screenshots surfacing:
- Runs 24/7 in the background, monitoring Gmail, Calendar, chat, browsing sessions, and location — no user prompt required.
- Supports recurring **Skills** (saved task templates) and scheduled workflows that run without oversight.
- Onboarding text states: *"Spark can share sensitive information with third parties or make purchases without asking for confirmation in some situations."*
- Stores remote browser session data including login state for background workflow continuity.
- Described by Android Authority as "gunning for Claude Cowork's throne."

Gemini Omni (unified text/image/video pipeline) also expected as the headline model announcement. I/O keynote: [io.google/2026](https://io.google/2026), May 19 10am PT.

Sources: [Android Authority — Spark onboarding leak](https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-spark-onboarding-screen-leaked-3667118/), [Android Authority — Cowork comparison](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-gemini-spark-agent-leak-3667475/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/14/gemini-spark-insight/), [nokiapoweruser.com](https://nokiapoweruser.com/google-io-2026-gemini-spark-omni-gemini-3-5-rumors/)

**Why it matters:** Gemini Spark is a direct feature-set competitor to Claude Cowork. The "purchases without confirmation" design is a deliberate usability stance that will invite security scrutiny — compare with Claude's explicit confirmation gates. Worth watching how Google positions this in the keynote tomorrow.

### [NEW] MCP 2026 roadmap: stateless transport, task lifecycle, governance, enterprise readiness
The Model Context Protocol team published its [2026 roadmap](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/), structured around Working Groups rather than dates. Four priority areas:

1. **Streamable HTTP stateless scaling** — Target: servers that work correctly behind load balancers and proxies without session-affinity hacks. Currently stateful sessions fight with horizontal scaling in production deployments.
2. **Tasks lifecycle** — Closing gaps surfaced in early production use: retry semantics for transient failures, expiry policies for result retention after task completion.
3. **Governance** — A Contributor Ladder SEP defining progression from community participant → WG contributor → lead maintainer → core maintainer; a delegation model allowing WGs with a track record to merge extension updates without full core-maintainer review.
4. **Enterprise readiness** — SSO-integrated auth, audit trails, and standard gateway patterns for production enterprise deployments.

Sources: [blog.modelcontextprotocol.io](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/), [thenewstack.io](https://thenewstack.io/model-context-protocol-roadmap-2026/), [modelcontextprotocol.io/development/roadmap](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/development/roadmap)

**Why it matters:** The stateless Streamable HTTP fix is the most important item for anyone building or deploying MCP servers in production. The governance changes mean extension authors get a faster merge path once their WG has a track record.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Meta Avocado delayed past May; internal benchmarks below GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.7
Meta's flagship proprietary model (codenamed Avocado) missed its May launch target — the third slip since the original late-2025 window. According to multiple reports, internal tests put Avocado between Gemini 2.5 and 3.0 in capability, below GPT-5.5 Instant and Claude Opus 4.7. Meta leadership reportedly discussed licensing Google's Gemini as a short-term stopgap; no decision was made.

Context: Avocado is a proprietary, closed-weights model — a deliberate departure from Meta's Llama open-source strategy. Meta has committed $115-135B in 2026 capex to AI infrastructure despite the delays.
