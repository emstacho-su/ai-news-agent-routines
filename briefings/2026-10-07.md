## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic Expands Startup Program at SF Tech Week Claude Founder House
Anthropic held a "Claude Founder House" event on October 6 as part of SF Tech Week, unveiling an expanded startup program: eligible early-stage founders get a free year of Claude Team (up to five seats) plus $1,000 in API credits. Eligibility requires equity funding from an institutional investor, founding within the last four years, and no prior Anthropic startup credits. The program was presented by Beth Robertson (Head of Startups) and Gaby Wilkerson-Melnick (Global Head of Startup Partnerships). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-gives-startups-a-free-year-of-enterprise-service-and-1000-in-token-credits/) | [Anthropic Events](https://www.anthropic.com/events/claude-founder-house-san-francisco)

**Why it matters:** If Stack ever spins up a Claude-based product with a co-founder, this covers the first year of Claude Team at zero cost — worth bookmarking the eligibility window.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Microsoft + NVIDIA: MAI Models Coming to Local Device — October 7 SF Event
Microsoft is hosting a Windows + Surface event in San Francisco today with CEO Satya Nadella, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, and EVP Pavan Davuluri. The agenda covers transitioning Microsoft's MAI model family from cloud-only inference to local on-device execution paired with NVIDIA RTX Spark silicon. The Surface Laptop Ultra (announced at Build 2026 in June) is the reference device. This is the first time Microsoft and NVIDIA have co-staged a joint on-device AI demo at this scale, positioning the full-stack MAI + RTX Spark combo as an offline-capable alternative to cloud APIs. [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/windows/324000/microsoft-to-hold-surface-and-windows-event-on-october-7) | [Forkast](https://forkast.news/microsofts-mai-models-were-built-for-the-cloud-october-7-is-when-they-come-to-the-device)

**Why it matters:** On-device inference matters for air-gapped or security-sensitive environments where cloud API calls aren't allowed. RTX Spark is NVIDIA's consumer-tier NPU answer to Apple Silicon's ANE.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Google DeepMind Releases EmbeddingGemma 2 (740M, Apache 2.0)
Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740-million-parameter multimodal embedding model under Apache 2.0. It encodes text, code, images, video, and audio into a single shared vector space. The permissive license means it's a practical drop-in for local RAG pipelines, vector search, and cross-modal retrieval without proprietary API dependencies. [AI Weekly / LLM Stats News](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

**Why it matters:** A frontier-lab multimodal embedding model with a truly open license is rare — most are API-gated or non-commercial. Directly useful for building local search and memory layers in agent systems.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] OpenAI Expands ChatGPT Ads to Image, Video, and Conversational Formats
OpenAI will begin testing richer visual ad formats in ChatGPT later this month in the US. New units include images, video, native placements, and a "conversational" ad where users can directly ask questions about a product. Rollout is limited to Free and Go ($8/mo) tiers; Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise stay ad-free. ChatGPT now reaches 1.2 billion people weekly. Brand safety integrations: DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science. Conversion measurement: Hightouch, Tealium, LiveRamp. [The Ad DB](https://www.theatdb.com/news/openai-gives-chatgpt-ads-a-visual-upgrade) | [Analysis](https://leovirlan.substack.com/p/openai-chatgpt-ads-launch-analysis)

### [NEW] Google Contracts 3,590 MW from Constellation Energy (25% New Nuclear) for AI Compute
Google signed a 3,590-MW power deal with Constellation Energy to support AI data center build-out, with roughly 25% of capacity coming from new nuclear generation across 11 reactor upgrades in Illinois, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey. This is one of the largest single corporate nuclear procurement agreements on record, following Microsoft's Three Mile Island restart deal in 2025. AI power demand — not consumer demand — is now the primary driver of utility-scale nuclear revival in the US. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
