## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.208: screen reader mode, vim bindings, corporate process wrapper

v2.1.208 published July 14 with 45 changes. Key additions:

- **Screen reader mode**: opt-in plain-text rendering. Enable via `claude --ax-screen-reader`, `CLAUDE_AX_SCREEN_READER=1`, or `"axScreenReader": true` in settings.
- **`vimInsertModeRemaps`**: map two-key insert sequences (e.g. `jj → Escape`) in vim mode — finally.
- **`CLAUDE_CODE_PROCESS_WRAPPER`**: agent view and the background service now run every Claude Code self-spawn through a required wrapper executable. Useful for corporate environments with a mandatory launcher (DLP wrappers, audit loggers, etc).
- **Mouse click support** for multi-select menus and "Other" input rows in fullscreen mode.
- **Bug fixes**: fast mode now auto-restores when switching back to a model that supports it; large markdown tables (200+ rows) no longer stall rendering — first 200 rows shown with a "… N more rows" notice; replies typed to background agents no longer silently dropped if delivery fails.

Sources: [Claude Code changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog), [Releasebot Claude Code](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code), [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)

### [NEW] Ben Bernanke joins Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust

Anthropic added Dr. Ben Bernanke — former Federal Reserve Chair (2006–2014), Nobel laureate in Economics (2022), current Brookings Distinguished Fellow — to the Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT) on July 9, 2026. The LTBT is Anthropic's independent oversight body with authority to appoint board members; it advises leadership on mission alignment and societal risk.

Bernanke's mandate is focused on AI's macroeconomic effects. He joins Neil Buddy Shah (global health), Richard Fontaine (national security), and Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar (law/policy).

**Why it matters:** Bernanke's Great Depression research centered on how financial-system failures cascade into real-economy harm. Anthropic is explicitly naming AI-driven economic disruption as a risk domain the LTBT now covers.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] TSMC posts all-time revenue record: AI demand has rewritten the semiconductor calendar

TSMC reported Q2 2026 revenue of T$1.27 trillion ($39.6B), up 36% YoY — a new all-time quarterly record. June alone came in at T$442.7B, +68% YoY, the largest monthly figure in the company's 38-year history.

- N3 (3nm) manufacturing and CoWoS (advanced AI packaging) are sold out through year-end.
- AI accounted for the majority of upside; Apple's strategic partnership (A-series and M-series SoCs) contributed on the consumer side.
- TSMC management noted demand visibility has extended further into 2027 than in any prior earnings cycle.

**Why it matters:** N3 is the process node on which most frontier AI training accelerators are built. "Sold out through year-end" means no new compute capacity becomes available in 2026 for anyone not already in the queue.

### [NEW] Google rationed Meta's Gemini compute — then leased SpaceX Colossus at $920M/month

The AI compute crunch is no longer theoretical. Two data points this cycle:

1. **Google capped Meta's Gemini API access** in March 2026, telling Meta it could not supply the requested compute volume. Google is simultaneously the supplier of API inference and a competing consumer of its own GPUs; when those two demands conflicted, enterprise customers got rationed.
2. **Google signed a deal with SpaceX** in June 2026 to pay approximately $920M/month — roughly $30B over the contract — for access to ~110,000 Nvidia GPUs at SpaceX's Colossus data centers (the same facility Anthropic rented in 2025). TSMC's sold-out packaging lines explain why Google couldn't simply order more chips.

Together these stories document a supply regime where even hyperscalers cannot buy their way to unconstrained capacity inside a single fiscal year.

Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-caps-meta-gemini-compute-shortage), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/06/29/google-limits-metas-gemini-usage-over-compute-shortages/), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4607892-google-rations-ai-capacity-to-meta-as-infrastructure-crunch-intensifies-ft), [AI Business Weekly](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/google-rationed-gemini-capacity-meta-compute-shortage-2026)

## Practical tips

- **If you use Claude Code in a corporate proxy environment**: v2.1.208's `CLAUDE_CODE_PROCESS_WRAPPER` is the clean way to inject a required launcher without patching the Claude binary or overriding Node's spawn behavior. Set it to the path of your wrapper executable and every Claude sub-process routes through it.
- **Screen reader mode in Claude Code**: enabled per-session via `--ax-screen-reader` or persistently in settings. Useful for accessibility tooling integrations even if you're not a screen reader user — the plain-text output stream is easier to parse programmatically.
