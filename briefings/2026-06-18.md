## Anthropic

### [NEW] Seoul office opens — third Asia-Pacific presence for Anthropic

Led by KiYoung Choi (30 years in Korean enterprise tech), the office comes with a wave of enterprise partnerships: LG CNS is rolling Claude out to thousands of employees across LG Group's software development workflows; Samsung SDS is deploying Claude enterprise-wide within Samsung Electronics; Nexon is using Claude Code for live-service game development. ## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Gemini CLI sunset executes today — migrate to Antigravity CLI (`agy`)

As of June 18, 2026, Google has stopped serving requests from Gemini CLI and Gemini Code Assist IDE extensions for consumer tiers: Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra, and free Gemini Code Assist for Individuals. The replacement is Antigravity CLI (`agy`), a closed-source Go rewrite announced at Google I/O 2026 alongside Antigravity 2.0. Feature parity is not 1:1 at launch — `agy` preserves Agent Skills, Hooks, Subagents, and Extensions (now called Antigravity plugins) but other Gemini CLI features are absent. Enterprise customers on Gemini Code Assist Standard/Enterprise and Google Cloud licenses are **unaffected** and retain full Gemini CLI access with ongoing model updates. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605) | [Groundy](https://groundy.com/articles/google-sunsets-gemini-cli-on-june-18-forced-migration-to-antigravity-cli-breaks/) | [aibuilderclub.com](https://www.aibuilderclub.com/blog/google-kills-gemini-cli-june-18-2026)

**Why it matters:** Any CI/CD pipeline or scripted workflow invoking `gemini` commands under a consumer Google AI account is broken as of today. Enterprise Gemini Code Assist users can ignore this.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Databricks launches Genie One: agentic AI coworker with live-context ontology

Databricks wrapped Data+AI Summit 2026 (Moscone Center, June 15-18, 30K+ attendees) by announcing Genie One, an AI coworker for business teams in marketing, finance, and sales. The core technical differentiation is Genie Ontology: a live context layer that continuously learns your business from internal and external data, keeping the agent grounded in current business state rather than a static snapshot. Genie One targets enterprise reporting, sales pipeline analysis, and finance reconciliation — positioning against Microsoft Copilot for Finance and Salesforce Einstein. The summit's other major outputs: OpenSharing (Linux Foundation open protocol for cross-org sharing of agent skills and ML models), Unity AI Gateway (runtime policy enforcement layer for models, agents, tools, and MCP servers), and the Agent Bricks milestone of 100K+ agents and 1+ quadrillion tokens/year.
