## Model Releases

### [NEW] Liquid AI LFM3: six open-weight 2B models for on-device deployment

Liquid AI released LFM3 on October 27, a family of six specialized ~2B-parameter models branching from a shared 1.73B base (LFM3-1.7B-Base, pretrained on 22T tokens). The family: LFM3-1.7B (text), LFM3-VL-2B (text+image+video → text), LFM3-Audio-2B (text/audio → text/speech), LFM3-Omni-2B (all modalities), and two decision models d1-2B and d1-omni-2B that return calibrated outputs rather than generated text. Architecture is hybrid: 24 layers mixing 13 gated short convolutions, 5 sliding-window attention layers (256-token window), and 6 global attention layers. Context: 256K tokens. Fits in ~1GB at 4-bit.

Company-reported decode speed: 1.8–2.8× faster than the next on-device model across tested hardware. Open-weight under LFM Open License v1.0 on Hugging Face and the Liquid AI API. Day-0 partners: Ambarella, AMD, Arm, Intel (OpenVINO), NVIDIA, OpenRouter, Qualcomm, Vercel.

Sources: [AIWeekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/liquid-ai-opens-six-model-lfm3-family-at-256k-context), [Digg](https://digg.com/tech/30t7xvd4), [Hermes AI](https://hermes-ai.net/news/liquid-ai-s-lfm-3-shrinks-six-specialized-models-to-run-on-your-phone/).

**Why it matters:** The hybrid conv/attention architecture is a direct competitor to pure-transformer edge models. Six specialized heads from one backbone is a deployment-friendly structure — relevant if you're building edge agent stacks or experimenting with on-device inference.

### [NEW] [RUMOR] Google Gemini 4 Carbon: internal build spotted on Chatbot Arena

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] NVIDIA $2,000/GPU/year: Jensen Huang floats agent software licensing fee

At GTC Berlin (Oct 22), Huang described licensing as "becoming a major part of the business" and cited ~$2,000/GPU/year as an example fee for running agent software in AI factories. NVIDIA has not published this as a pricing announcement and has not clarified which products or SKUs it would apply to. Existing AI Enterprise already lists at $4,500/year per GPU; the $2,000 figure is below current single-GPU enterprise software pricing. With ~5M deployed enterprise GPUs, $2,000/unit represents ~$10B/year in potential software revenue.

Sources: [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/nvidia-plans-to-charge-2000-usd-per-year-for-ai-agents), [AIWeekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/huang-floats-2000-per-gpu-yearly-fee-for-nvidia-agent-software).

**Why it matters:** If NVIDIA starts bundling agent software licensing with its hardware sales, the cost model for self-hosted agentic infrastructure (vs. managed services like Routines) shifts materially. Worth tracking when NVIDIA formalizes this.
