## Research & Technical

### [NEW] OpenAI "Astra" solves ten open math/CS problems; one headed to Annals of Mathematics
OpenAI's internal model "Astra" (not publicly released or documented) solved ten previously open problems in mathematics and computer science. Fields Medalist Timothy Gowers reviewed one result and endorsed it for submission to *Annals of Mathematics* — one of the field's most selective journals. Announced August 1, 2026. No technical report or model card published.

**Why it matters:** If the Gowers endorsement leads to publication, it's the first documented case of an AI-generated result reaching a top-tier pure math journal — a qualitatively different bar from prior AI math work on competition problems (AMC/AIME/IMO). "Astra" is also an undocumented model string, raising questions about what capability tier this represents.

Sources: [AI News Today August 2 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-august-2-2026), [AI News August 2026 — aitoolsrecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/AINewsaugust2026.aspx)

### [NEW] Google DeepMind opens $10M multi-agent safety research funding call
DeepMind, Schmidt Sciences, the Cooperative AI Foundation, the Advanced Research and Invention Agency (ARIA), and Google.org are jointly funding up to $10M for research on behavior and risk mitigation in large-scale multi-agent systems. Application deadline: August 8, 2026. Awardees announced Autumn 2026. Framing: "millions of AI agents built by different organizations interacting across digital environments."

**Why it matters:** First major coordinated safety funding call targeting multi-agent interaction risk specifically — distinct from single-model alignment. The Aug 8 deadline is five days away.

Sources: [DeepMind — Investing in multi-agent AI safety research](https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/)
