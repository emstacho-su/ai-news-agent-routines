## Anthropic

### [NEW] First profitable quarter projected: $10.9B Q2 revenue, $559M operating income

Anthropic told investors on May 20 that it expects $10.9B in Q2 2026 revenue — up 130% from $4.8B in Q1 — and is on pace for its first-ever operating profit of $559M. The compute cost ratio is projected to drop from 71 cents per dollar of revenue in Q1 to 56 cents in Q2. The company flagged that profitability may not persist through the full year due to large committed compute spend coming online later in 2026, but the directional shift is significant: internal projections last summer pegged full-year profitability no earlier than 2028. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-20/anthropic-on-pace-for-first-profitable-quarter-as-revenue-surges), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-says-its-about-to-have-its-first-profitable-quarter/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)

**Why it matters:** The compute ratio improvement is the number to watch — it shows Anthropic is getting more efficient at the same time it's scaling, which changes the IPO narrative considerably.

### [NEW] Anthropic acquires Stainless for $300M+, winding down hosted SDK product

Anthropic acquired Stainless, the SDK auto-generation startup founded by former Stripe engineer Alex Rattray, for more than $300M. Stainless was used by OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare, Replicate, and Runway to auto-generate and maintain language-specific SDKs. All hosted Stainless products, including the SDK generator, are being wound down; existing customers retain full rights to SDKs already generated. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless), [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/05/19/anthropic-acquires-sdk-startup-stainless-for-over-300m-cutting-off-key-tool-used-by-openai-and-google/)

**Why it matters:** This is infrastructure denial as competitive strategy — OpenAI and Google now need to rebuild or migrate their SDK tooling pipelines. For anyone building SDKs on top of Claude APIs, the Stainless-generated Python/TypeScript SDKs they already own are unaffected, but future SDK maintenance tooling needs to shift elsewhere.

### [NEW] Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic pre-training team

Andrej Karpathy (OpenAI co-founder, former Tesla AI lead, "Software 3.0" advocate) announced he is joining Anthropic's pre-training team, starting this week. His mandate includes building a new team focused on using Claude itself to accelerate pre-training research — automating parts of the research loop. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/19/anthropic-openai-karpathy-andrej-claude), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/anthropic-hires-openai-cofounder-andrej-karpathy-former-tesla-ai-lead.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/)

**Why it matters:** Karpathy's framing of "Software 3.0" — code as agent intelligence substrate — aligns directly with Claude Code and Routines' direction. His pre-training mandate signals Anthropic is pushing toward AI-accelerated AI training, which has downstream implications for model cadence.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.146: /code-review command, MCP pagination fix, Windows PowerShell fix

v2.1.146 shipped with several targeted fixes. `/simplify` is renamed to `/code-review` and now accepts an optional effort level (e.g. `/code-review high`). Auto mode no longer suppresses `AskUserQuestion` when a user or skill explicitly relies on it. Key bug fixes: Windows PowerShell regression (v2.1.124 regression causing "command line is invalid" with winget/Store-installed `pwsh`); MCP servers returning paginated `resources/list`, `resources/templates/list`, and `prompts/list` were silently dropping items past page 1; full-screen strobing in Windows Terminal for attached background sessions during streaming. Diff rendering performance improved for large edits. [Claude Code releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases), [Changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)

**Why it matters:** The MCP pagination fix is a real correctness bug — if you have a paginated MCP resource server, you were silently getting incomplete tool lists. Worth re-checking any MCP server that exposes many resources.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Musk loses OpenAI lawsuit: all claims dismissed by jury in under two hours (May 19)

A federal jury in Oakland unanimously rejected all of Elon Musk's claims against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman on May 19, deliberating less than two hours after eleven days of testimony. The core finding: all claims were barred by the statute of limitations — Musk waited too long after knowing the facts before filing in 2024. Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers accepted and adopted the jury's findings. Musk's legal team vowed to appeal. Musk had sought up to $134B in damages and Altman's ouster, alleging OpenAI abandoned its nonprofit founding mission. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/18/nx-s1-5822366/musk-altman-openai-jury-verdict-claims-dismissed), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/18/tech/openai-musk-lawsuit-verdict), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-elon-musk-case-verdict-rcna345655)

### [NEW] Pope Leo XIV's AI encyclical "Magnifica Humanitas" launches May 25 alongside Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah

Pope Leo XIV will personally present his first encyclical "Magnifica Humanitas" (Magnificent Humanity) on May 25, centered on "the protection of the human person in the age of artificial intelligence." Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah — whose interpretability research is foundational to the current AI safety debate — will join the Vatican presentation alongside Curial cardinals and theologians. The joint launch is unusual; popes rarely present their own encyclicals. ## Model Releases
