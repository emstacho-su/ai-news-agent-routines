## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic compute commitments reach ~$517B over 11 months as Lambda deal closes

Anthropic signed a $35B six-year cloud deal with Nvidia-backed Lambda Labs on August 31, anchored at a Hut 8 data center in Nueces County, Texas. Nvidia serves as an intermediary in the capacity chain — it leases the facility from Hut 8 and Lambda pays Nvidia for access. Combined with earlier 2026 contracts — AWS ($100B/10yr), Fluidstack ($50B), Nscale ($45B), and SpaceX ($45B) — Anthropic's publicly disclosed 2026 compute spend exceeds $275B in announced deals. The Information reported September 6 that total compute capacity reservations since October 2025 reach as much as $517B, though the majority plays out over the coming decade via multiyear take-or-pay agreements rather than immediate outflows. Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/anthropic-seals-35-billion-cloud-deal-with-nvidia-backed-lambda), [TechCrunch/Nscale](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/anthropic-continues-compute-gobbling-streak-in-45-billion-deal-with-nscale/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-anthropic-lambda-35-billion-cloud-deal/)

**Why it matters:** These reservations were made before Anthropic has raised equivalent capital, meaning IPO proceeds plus continued Amazon/Google equity are already spoken for. For Claude Platform developers: capacity commitments at this scale are a concrete indicator that throughput and SLA headroom improve post-IPO rather than tighten.

### [UPDATE] Anthropic walks away from $6B Decart acquisition after due diligence

Bloomberg reported September 8 that Anthropic completed due diligence on Israeli startup Decart AI and decided not to proceed with the ~$6B deal. Decart builds software to optimize chip utilization during inference and training; the intended acquisition would have added Decart's team to Anthropic's inference performance group. Both parties may still explore non-acquisition partnerships. The collapse follows a pattern of Anthropic concentrating M&A-scale capital on capacity reservations rather than software acquisitions. Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/anthropic-said-to-walk-away-from-6-billion-decart-acquisition), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-walks-away-decart-6bn-acquisition), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-ends-talks-potential-6bn-113759986.html)

## Research & Technical

### [UPDATE] Google DeepMind WeatherNext 3: 5km resolution, hourly forecasts, raw satellite training

Google DeepMind launched WeatherNext 3 on September 3-4, integrating it into Google Search, Maps, Earth Engine, and the Maps Platform Weather API. The architectural shift: WN3 trains directly on raw satellite observations, eliminating the six-hour data lag that physical weather model outputs imposed on every predecessor. Resolution improves from 25km to 5km on surface temperature and moisture; refresh cadence moves from six-hour to hourly — five times sharper, six times more frequent. WN3 achieves 60% better rain prediction accuracy vs. WeatherNext 2, and outperforms ECMWF, Microsoft, and Nvidia on OperationalWeatherBench. Sources: [Google DeepMind blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-deepmind-launches-weathernext-3-with-hourly-5-kilometer-forecasts/), [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/09/04/google-deepmind-releases-weathernext-3-its-most-accurate-ai-weather-forecasting-model-yet/)

**Why it matters:** Training end-to-end on raw sensor data rather than physical model output is the same architecture decision that unlocked LLM performance gains in NLP — removing the human-engineered intermediate representation. WeatherNext 3 demonstrates the approach transfers directly to geophysical data. Same pattern will matter for any domain where ML has been trained on processed outputs rather than raw measurements.
