## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic Publishes 154-Page Threat Report Covering Bio, Cyber, Distillation Misuse

Anthropic released a 154-page threat report covering nine months of misuse it detected and disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026. The report groups cases under seven categories: cyber, influence campaigns, surveillance, fraud, biology, conventional weapons, and distillation. On the biological side, Anthropic flagged ~35 distinct research efforts with potentially weapons-relevant activity, including gain-of-function prompts, bird flu research, and novel venoms/toxins — the company calls bio "one of the most serious risks of frontier AI models." The distillation section is arguably the most operationally notable: Alibaba ran the largest undisclosed distillation campaign Anthropic has measured, peaking near 3 million Claude exchanges per day through more than 3,500 fake accounts, exposing user names, company files, and credentials to Claude without those users' knowledge. Sources: [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/09/10/health/anthropic-bioweapons-report), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/sep/10/anthropic-says-blocked-misuse-ai-could-supported-biological-weapons/), [The National News](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/09/10/anthropic-report-biological-weapons-ai/), [ResultSense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-09-11-anthropic-threat-report-distillation-bio-weapons/).

**Why it matters:** The Alibaba distillation case is a direct supply-chain trust problem for anyone building on top of third-party Claude integrations — Anthropic is using this report to pressure the ecosystem to enforce disclosure when Claude is in the stack.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] OpenAI Agents API: Codex Harness in Public Beta

OpenAI opened its Agents API to public beta on September 10. The API puts the managed Codex orchestration layer — sessions, context compaction, tool dispatch, subagent coordination, and crash recovery — behind a single programmable interface. Developers supply tools and choose execution environments; OpenAI handles the harness. Deployment options include OpenAI-managed sandboxes or bring-your-own-infrastructure, with launch integrations from Cloudflare, DigitalOcean, and Oracle. No additional fees beyond usage. Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/openai-launches-the-agents-api-in-public-beta-putting-the-codex-harness-behind-one-api-call/).

**Why it matters:** This is a direct parallel to Anthropic's Managed Agents (which shipped the same session-budget and multi-environment controls in August–September). Both providers now offer a "bring your tools, we manage the loop" primitive. Worth comparing posture: OpenAI exposes harness orchestration; Anthropic packages it as a Routine with `budget_reached` stop reasons and advisor models.

### [NEW] Positron AI Raises $875M at $5B Valuation for Asimov Inference Chip

Reno-based Positron closed a $375M Series C at a $3.5B pre-money valuation, with a follow-on Series C-1 of up to $500M anchored by NEA and Netscape co-founder Jim Clark, totaling $875M raised. The company's Asimov chip takes a memory-bandwidth-first approach: it uses commodity LPDDR5X (288 GB–2,304 GB per chip), bypassing HBM supply constraints and advanced packaging bottlenecks. Asimov is scheduled to tape out on TSMC N3P at end of 2026 with production in H2 2027. Titan, a multi-chip inference system combining four to eight Asimov dies, is also in planning. Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/10/chipmaker-positron-nabs-875m-to-speed-up-inference-with-consumer-grade-memory/), [Converge Digest](https://convergedigest.com/positron-ai-raises-875m-asimov-inference-silicon/), [Forkast](https://forkast.news/positron-ais-875m-bet-commodity-memory-could-break-nvidias-inference-lock/).

**Why it matters:** The memory-first inference bet is interesting architecturally — transformer inference is bandwidth-bound, not compute-bound for most workloads, so large commodity DRAM per chip could trade FLOPs for throughput on long-context and MoE models. TSMC N3P tapeout late 2026 means production won't arrive until late 2027 at earliest.

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] OpenAI's 10,000-Agent Swarm Claims Navier-Stokes Proof — With Provenance Dispute

On September 8, OpenAI reported that an internal experimental system running 10,000 agents over 88 hours produced a proposed proof of finite-time blowup for the forced 3D Navier-Stokes equations — one of the seven Clay Millennium Prize Problems, carrying a $1M prize. The result argues that a fluid vortex can reach infinite speed in finite time while retaining finite total energy. The proof was subsequently formalized in Lean (computer-verified). However, a provenance controversy has emerged: NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge were independently working on a closely related fluid-dynamics result and had used AI tools during their research; questions have been raised about whether de-identified data from their work influenced OpenAI's model. OpenAI says it did not access their specific work prior to publication. Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/09/09/openai-says-10-000-ai-agents-solved-a-usd1-million-math-problem-now-mathematicians-are-fighting), [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/09/business/openai-millennium-problems-navier-stokes-hnk), [Forkast](https://forkast.news/openais-10000-agent-navier-stokes-claim-solves-the-wrong-problem-and-the-right-one-has-a-provenance-controversy/).

**Why it matters:** If the Lean formalization holds peer review, this is the largest verifiable mathematical result produced by an autonomous agent system. The provenance angle matters regardless of the outcome — attribution of AI-assisted proofs is an unresolved problem for academic publishing and IP law.

## Practical tips

- **Distillation trust hygiene**: Anthropic's report makes explicit that some API resellers are routing traffic through Claude without disclosure. If you're using a third-party wrapper that claims to use a different model, verify you're not inadvertently in a data-exposure chain.
- **Agents API**: OpenAI's Agents API beta is free beyond usage — worth spinning up a test harness now if you're evaluating managed orchestration primitives alongside Anthropic's Managed Agents.
- **Navier-Stokes / Lean**: The formalizer stack (OpenAI's agent → Lean) is a real pattern for high-rigor verification tasks. Even if this specific result gets contested, the workflow (agentic generation + formal verifier) is immediately applicable to protocol verification and type-level proofs.
