## Anthropic

### [NEW] Claude Opus 4.8: Dynamic Workflows, cheaper /fast mode, better alignment (May 28)

Claude Opus 4.8 shipped May 28 across the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry (1M token context everywhere except Foundry at 200K). Pricing holds at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens—same as Opus 4.7. Headline benchmark improvement: **69.2% SWE-bench Pro** (vs Opus 4.7's 64.3%), a documented four-fold reduction in unreported code flaws, and better alignment scores on honesty and deception probes. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-8) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-releases-opus-4-8-with-new-dynamic-workflow-tool/)

**Dynamic Workflows** (research preview, Max/Team/Enterprise; requires Claude Code v2.1.154): describe a task and Claude generates a JavaScript orchestration script that fans work out to up to 16 concurrent subagents, capped at 1,000 total per run. Intermediate results live in script variables rather than Claude's context window—the main session stays responsive while agents work and the plan converges iteratively. Trigger: include the word "workflow" anywhere in your prompt, or enable **ultracode** (combines xhigh reasoning effort with automatic orchestration). [Claude docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/workflows) | [Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/introducing-dynamic-workflows-in-claude-code) | [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-ships-claude-opus-4-8-alongside-dynamic-workflows-and-cheaper-fast-mode-with-workflows-capped-at-1000-subagents/)

**/fast mode re-priced:** $10/$50 per million tokens, down from $30/$150 with Opus 4.7—a 3× reduction. Effort controls (how much thinking Claude does per task) also now surface on claude.ai for non-CLI sessions. [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/claude-opus-48-release/)

**Why it matters:** The JS-script-as-orchestration-plan approach is architecturally distinct from the existing Agent Teams and Managed Agents patterns—state lives outside the conversation window, which sidesteps context bloat on large-scale tasks. The 1,000-subagent cap and 16-concurrent limit are the meaningful constraints to design around.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Gemini 3 Pro + Deep Think: 45.1% ARC-AGI-2, 1501 LMArena Elo (May 28, preview)

Google released **Gemini 3** in preview on May 28, positioned as their flagship multimodal model above Gemini 3.5 Flash. Standard Gemini 3 Pro tops LMArena at 1501 Elo, scores 37.5% on Humanity's Last Exam (no tools), and 91.9% GPQA Diamond. [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3/)

**Deep Think mode** pushes substantially further: **41.0% Humanity's Last Exam**, **93.8% GPQA Diamond**, **45.1% ARC-AGI-2** (with code execution, ARC Prize Verified). The ARC-AGI-2 score is the headline—the benchmark was explicitly designed to resist current-generation frontier models, and 45.1% is a meaningful jump. Google also reports gold-medal-level results on written sections of the 2025 International Physics and Chemistry Olympiads. Deep Think initially gated to safety testers before wider Ultra subscriber rollout. [Google Deep Think blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-deep-think/) | [BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/models/gemini-3-pro-deep-think)

API pricing for Gemini 3 Pro is unconfirmed while in preview. Gemini 3.5 Flash ($1.50/$9.00/M) remains the primary developer model.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] California AI bills: 30 advance through crossover deadline (May 29)

Nearly all of California's 30 AI-related bills cleared their chamber of origin ahead of today's May 29 crossover deadline and move to the other chamber for committee and floor votes. Notable: **AB 1609** (customer service chatbot disclosure requirements, 68-0 Assembly vote), **AB 1651** (AI use in State Bar exam administration), **SB 243/264** (AI disclosure for simulated-human conversation tools), **SB 994** (algorithmic rent-setting). California AI law has historically set de facto national standards; the pipeline volume in 2026 is roughly 3× 2025. [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-may29-2026)
