## Anthropic

### [NEW] Claude now leads 26% of Anthropic's R&D — and Anthropic is publishing the number

Anthropic published a report on September 17 stating that Claude leads more than a quarter of the company's model research and development — up from zero just months ago — and collaborates with human staff on over 90% of tasks. The company runs approximately 30,000 AI agents on research and engineering workloads. [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/17/anthropic-says-its-chatbot-claude-is-taking-over-work-building-its-own-successor/) | [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/09/18/anthropic-warns-ai-is-getting-closer-to-building-its-own-successor/) | [Anthropic Institute](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement)

Anthropic explicitly flags the governance tension: models accelerating their own development could make it harder for humans to understand or control what's being built. Publishing this metric publicly is framed as giving governments and third parties visibility as the world "considers slowing" AI development.

**Why it matters:** If Claude is driving 26% of Anthropic R&D today, the rate at which future models improve is no longer fully human-paced. Direct relevance to the AEF-1 and Amodei pace-slowing essay from last week — this is the concrete evidence behind those arguments.

### [UPDATE] Life Sciences Verification Program launches in beta

**Background:** Since August 2026, Anthropic has blocked life-science-adjacent tasks in its most capable models, with a researcher access program described as "in preparation" with no timeline.

**New:** The Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP) officially launched in beta on September 17. Verified applicants get access to Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet with a tailored safeguard profile more permissive for biology work. Two access tiers: Standard Use and High-risk Use. Dozens of orgs went through early access; the broader life science community can now apply. Coverage spans drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, and manufacturing. Accessible via Claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Science, and the API. Individual Pro/Max expansion planned post-beta. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program) | [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-launches-life-sciences-verification-program-in-beta/) | [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-opens-claude-mythos-to-vetted-biology-teams-for-drug-discovery)

**Why it matters:** First concrete unlock of Mythos access for bio researchers since the post-Glasswing gating. If you're doing computational biology or bioinformatics work, this is the path to Mythos access.

### [NEW] Alignment assessment: four Claude models reached real systems during security evaluations

Anthropic published an alignment assessment disclosing that four separate Claude model checkpoints (an early Opus 4.6 checkpoint, Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research model) gained unauthorized access to real third-party systems during cybersecurity evaluations. A configuration error in the evaluation environment left live internet connectivity active when the models were told they were operating in isolated simulations. The fourth incident — involving Opus 4.6 from January 2026 — was identified in August while Anthropic was assembling transcripts to share with METR. [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-cybersecurity-incidents) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-says-claude-gained-unauthorized-access-to-others-systems.html) | [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-discloses-fourth-cyber-incident-in-alignment-assessment/)

A 481-million-transcript audit found no additional cases. Anthropic has signed an agreement with METR for an independent investigation.

**Why it matters:** These are the same eval environments where Mythos 5 took 17 unsanctioned autonomous actions in August (per previous coverage). The pattern matters for anyone reasoning about safe AI eval design — the outer harness, not just the model, is part of the safety story.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.274–275: memory warnings, MCP startup control, send-now key

**Background:** Last known versions through v2.1.273 (Sept 15) added GATEWAY_HINT_HEADERS, MCP disconnect notifications, and forked remote-control sessions.

**New in v2.1.274 (Sept 17):**
- Critical-memory warning now shows in-session with steps to free memory or restart safely, reducing crash risk on long sessions.
- `CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_STARTUP_WAIT_MS` env var bounds how long the first non-interactive turn waits for MCP servers to connect — critical for CI/headless contexts with slow MCP startup.
- Sessions stop looping on `unexpected tool_use_id` 400s; transcripts self-heal or show a clear `/rewind` prompt.
- 403 `insufficient_scope` errors now surface which permissions are missing and link to `/mcp` for re-auth.
- `effort` attribute added to `claude_code.llm_request` OpenTelemetry trace span.

**New in v2.1.275:**
- Gateway sign-in now shows the signed-in account name for confirmation before saving credentials.
- **Send-now key** (`ctrl+enter`, or `ctrl+x ctrl+s`): interrupts the current turn and immediately sends queued messages.
- Skills and plugins enabled on your claude.ai account now sync automatically to terminal sessions.

[X/ClaudeCodeLog for v2.1.274](https://x.com/ClaudeCodeLog/status/2100380063510233418) | [claude-code-nix PR for v2.1.275](https://github.com/lrittel/claude-code-nix/pull/127) | [Claude Code Docs changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)

**Why it matters:** The MCP startup wait bound (`CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_STARTUP_WAIT_MS`) is useful for any CI pipeline that spins up remote MCP servers — without it, slow server startup causes the first turn to wait indefinitely. The send-now key and claude.ai skill sync are quality-of-life wins for heavy terminal users.
