## Anthropic

### [NEW] Claude for Small Business: 15 workflows, 15 skills, 8 connectors, city tour starts today

Launched May 13, Anthropic's small business bundle plugs Claude into QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365 as a single toggle install. The 15 bundled workflows cover payroll planning, month-end close, invoice chasing, lead triage, contract review, and marketing campaign management; the 15 reusable skills extend into cash-flow forecasting and content strategy. Anthropic partnered with PayPal on a free on-demand AI Fluency for Small Business course, and kicks off a free half-day workshop tour across 10 US cities — Chicago first, today. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-small-business), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-courts-a-new-kind-of-customer-small-business-owners/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-claude-small-business-smb).

**Why it matters:** The same plugin/skill packaging from the finance (May 5) and legal (May 12) verticals is now targeting the SMB long tail. The MCP connector schema and skill structure are worth watching if you build tooling for that market.

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.141: 61 changes — terminalSequence hooks, workspace identity federation, Rewind compression

**Background:** v2.1.139-140 shipped /goal, agent view dashboard, and case-insensitive subagent_type matching.

**New (v2.1.141):** `terminalSequence` field added to hook JSON output so hooks can emit desktop notifications, window titles, and bells without a controlling terminal. `ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID` env var enables workload identity federation — minted tokens are now scopable to a specific workspace, useful for enterprise multi-team deployments. `claude agents --cwd <path>` filters the session list to a directory. `/feedback` can now attach recent sessions (last 24 h or last 7 days) for cross-session bug reports. Rewind menu gains "Summarize up to here" to compress earlier context while preserving recent turns intact — useful when you are mid-session and burning context fast.

Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code), [Claude Code Changelog](https://claudefa.st/blog/guide/changelog).

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Google Gemini Intelligence: proactive cross-app AI for Android, Googlebook laptops, I/O keynote May 19

At the Android Show pre-I/O event on May 12, Google introduced Gemini Intelligence — its umbrella brand for proactive AI that operates across Android apps without waiting for commands. Demonstrated capabilities: pulling from Gmail/Calendar to build shopping lists and book reservations; "auto browse" in Chrome for agentic web navigation; AI-generated widgets via natural language; Android Auto that uses message and calendar context for voice-driven replies. Google also introduced "Googlebook," a new laptop category where Gemini Intelligence is a first-class OS feature; first Googlebooks from Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo arrive this fall. Rollout: Pixel and Samsung phones this summer, broader Android this fall. The full Google I/O 2026 keynote (May 19-20) is expected to include a new Gemini model and developer-facing agent tooling. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2171038/everything-announced-at-android-show-google-io-2026/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/everything-google-announced-at-its-android-show-from-googlebooks-to-vibe-coded-widgets/).

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] Trump-Xi summit: US clears H200 exports to ~10 Chinese firms; Beijing blocks actual delivery

**Background:** Trump arrived in Beijing May 13 with AI chip policy as the dominant tech agenda item; Jensen Huang joined the delegation last-minute.

**New:** The US cleared Nvidia H200 chip sales to roughly 10 Chinese technology firms including Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, and JD.com. Despite the clearance, no deliveries have materialized: Beijing is steering those buyers toward domestic Huawei alternatives to avoid creating dependency on foreign hardware. Both governments agreed to a "constructive China-US relationship of strategic stability" as a framework for the next three years. Samsung and SK Hynix rose pre-market on the chip optimism signal even as the delivery picture remains murky.

Sources: [CNBC (H200 clearance)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/trump-xi-meeting-china-stocks-ai-rally.html), [CNBC (summit framework)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/trump-xi-summit-us-china-trade-taiwan-iran-nvidia.html), [WION (China blocks delivery)](https://www.wionews.com/world/nvidia-h200-china-ai-chip-sale-blocked-1778749708827/amp).
