### [NEW] Microsoft open-sources RAMPART + Clarity: agentic AI safety for CI/CD (May 20)

RAMPART (Risk Assessment and Measurement Platform for Agentic Red Teaming) is a pytest-native framework built on Microsoft's PyRIT toolkit. It lets teams encode adversarial and benign agent scenarios — prompt injection, out-of-bounds tool use, behavioral drift — as repeatable tests that run in CI. The standout design choice: statistical trial policies ("this action must be safe in at least 80% of runs") account for model probabilistic behavior rather than requiring deterministic pass/fail.

Clarity is the companion for the pre-code phase: a structured AI design reviewer that guides problem clarification, failure-mode analysis, and decision tracking before implementation begins. Both are open-source on GitHub.

**Why it matters:** If you're shipping Claude Code routines or any production agentic workflow, RAMPART is the first framework that plugs agentic red-teaming directly into your CI pipeline without bespoke infrastructure. Practical for this project's Phase R7 deployment gating.

### [NEW] OpenAI joins C2PA + SynthID: dual-layer provenance on all generated images (May 19)

OpenAI joined the C2PA standard steering committee and embedded Google DeepMind's SynthID invisible watermark alongside existing C2PA Content Credentials on all images from ChatGPT, Codex, and the API. Rationale for the dual layer: C2PA metadata carries signed origin and edit history but gets stripped on format conversion; SynthID embeds a pixel-level signal that survives re-uploads and screenshots.

Public verification tool live at openai.com/verify — upload any image to check whether C2PA credentials or a SynthID watermark are present, even if the metadata was stripped.

**Why it matters:** OpenAI and Google aligning on C2PA + SynthID as a shared stack is the clearest signal yet these two standards will be the industry floor for AI image attribution. Relevant context: SynthID has also been adopted by Kakao and Eleven Labs as noted in Google I/O coverage.

Sources: [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/advancing-content-provenance/), [resultsense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-05-20-openai-c2pa-synthid-content-provenance/)

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Armada raises $230M Series B at $2B valuation for modular deployable AI data centers (May 19)

Armada builds self-contained modular data center units deployable in days, running on local power sources — currently in production with the US Navy and remote offshore oil rigs. The oversubscribed Series B was co-led by Overmatch, 8090 Industries, and BlackRock; Johnson Controls also invested and will manufacture units at Galleon Forge One, a new 400,000 sq ft Arizona factory dedicated to production.

Scale: customer bookings grew 540% FY25→FY26; Q1 FY27 alone posted ~2,000% YoY growth as demand from defense, energy, and industrial customers accelerates.

**Why it matters:** The long tail of AI deployment is edge and classified environments where hyperscale cloud is off-limits. Armada is building the infrastructure layer for that segment — and the funding scale and growth numbers signal this is moving from niche to mainstream faster than expected.
