## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Claude Fable 5: pricing confirmed at 2× Opus, 30-day retention mandate triggers backlash

**Background:** Fable 5 launched June 9 on Mythos-class weights with benchmarks headlining 95.0% SWE-bench Verified; free to paid subscribers through a limited promotional window.

**New:**
- **API pricing confirmed:** $10/1M input tokens, $50/1M output tokens — exactly double Opus 4.8, making Fable 5 the most expensive generally available frontier model. Free access under Pro/Max/Team/seat-based Enterprise expires **June 22**; starting June 23, usage bills from the programmatic credit pool at these full API rates.
- **Mandatory 30-day data retention:** Anthropic has added a blanket 30-day retention policy to all Fable 5 API traffic, explicitly overriding prior zero-retention enterprise agreements. Stated purpose: defending against novel attacks and jailbreaks, not model training. The practical effect for GDPR-bound organizations and regulated industries (finance, healthcare, legal) is that Fable 5 is effectively off-limits until Anthropic offers compliant carve-outs.
- **Developer backlash:** A notable cross-community pushback has emerged. Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue used the launch to argue that capability centralization is the core AI risk and called for renewed focus on open science. Enterprise buyers are raising compliance flags. On the positive side, analytics company Hex reported Fable 5 hit 90% on their complex long-running analytical task benchmark — the first model to reach that mark.

**Why it matters:** At $50/1M output tokens, a Routine or Agent SDK workflow generating 50K-token responses costs $2.50/run at Fable 5 rates. Your Max 20× plan credits ($200/mo) cover roughly 80 such runs before you're out of budget for the month. Evaluate Fable 5 now during the free window; the data retention policy is the harder constraint for anything with compliance requirements.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] OpenAI + Oracle Cloud: enterprise Universal Credits cover GPT models and Codex

Enterprise Oracle customers can now apply Oracle Universal Credits toward OpenAI models (including GPT-5.5 Instant) and Codex — no separate OpenAI purchasing agreement required. The integration went live June 10–11 and is the first cloud-commitment pathway for OpenAI alongside existing AWS and Azure options. For organizations already under Oracle spend commitments, this removes a procurement blocker for OpenAI adoption.

Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-on-oracle-cloud/), [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-teams-up-with-oracle-cloud)

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] WWDC 2026: MLX + Hugging Face models run through Foundation Models API

**Background:** WWDC 2026 runs June 8–12. The June 8–9 keynote and PSOTU established the LanguageModel protocol and Claude/Gemini/ChatGPT swap support via SPM dependency.

**New (developer sessions, June 10–11):** Apple has confirmed that MLX is now a first-class citizen in the Foundation Models stack, meaning developers can route any Hugging Face open-weight model through the same `LanguageModel` API that routes to Claude, Gemini, or Apple's on-device model. This unifies local and cloud inference behind one Swift interface — the same session code runs against a quantized Llama or Mistral model on-device or against Claude remotely with no changes. The WWDC session content is available at developer.apple.com.

Sources: [Medium / Varun Nuthalapati](https://medium.com/@nuthalapativarun/mlx-is-now-a-first-class-citizen-in-apples-ai-stack-run-any-hugging-face-model-through-foundation-9dfb8dad2191), [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/241/)

**Why it matters:** If you're building iOS/macOS tools, this is the cleanest local-vs-cloud routing story yet — no framework swap, just a dependency change.
