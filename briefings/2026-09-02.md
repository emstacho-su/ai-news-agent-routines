## Anthropic

### [NEW] Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1: released September 1, 2026

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on September 1, 2026. They are the same underlying model: Fable 5.1 is the general-availability version with Anthropic's production safeguards, while Mythos 5.1 is the restricted-access variant for vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations. Benchmarks show it outperforms Fable 5, Opus 5, and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol across coding and knowledge-work tasks. API pricing holds at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, but cache-read pricing dropped 75% to $0.25/M tokens (vs. $1.00/M on Fable 5). Zero data retention is now available on both Fable 5 and Fable 5.1 while EFS (below) rolls out.

**Why it matters:** The 75% cache read price cut directly hits agentic workloads with large, reused system prompts — exactly the pattern Stack's routines use. Test whether rewriting daily prompts for aggressive prompt caching lowers the Max plan token burn.

### [NEW] Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS): zero-data-retention + misuse detection, simultaneously

Announced September 1-2, EFS stores the activity data used for misuse detection in customer-controlled cloud storage (Amazon S3, Azure Blob, or Google Cloud Storage) under the customer's own encryption keys and access policies — not Anthropic's. This breaks the previous either/or: enterprises that needed ZDR compliance had to forfeit cross-session misuse detection. EFS delivers both. Co-developed with 100+ enterprise customers across financial services, healthcare, telecom, law, and the public sector. Rolling out in phases starting later this fall; eligible customers get ZDR on Fable 5/5.1 as a bridge.

Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-frontier-safeguards), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/02/anthropic-enterprise-frontier-safeguards/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/02/anthropic-enterprise-frontier-safeguards-efs/)

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] McKinsey State of AI 2026: a third of enterprises skipping software purchases

McKinsey's annual survey (published late August 2026) finds 32% of organizations have skipped buying at least one software product or feature because they can build it internally with agentic coding tools. Among "high performers" (the 6% attributing 5%+ of EBIT to AI), the skip rate is nearly 50%. Large enterprises scaling agents in one or more functions rose from 27% to 40% year-on-year. The shadow statistic: only 17% of organizations have actually deployed agents in production, meaning most of the 40% "scaling" is still in pilot or staging.

**Why it matters:** Stack's v1-routines rebuild is the practitioner version of this thesis — drop the hand-rolled agent stack, lean on managed primitives. McKinsey is now framing this as the mainstream enterprise decision, not an edge case.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Alibaba WAN 3.0: 30-second 1080p video + audio in one pass (missed Aug 24 release)

Alibaba launched WAN 3.0 on August 24 (public beta from Aug 6). It generates native 30-second, 30fps clips at up to 1080p with synchronized audio in a single generation pass. Unusual input support: documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, and webpages can be fed directly as source material and converted to video. Available via Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and Qwen Cloud. Pricing: $0.05/s at 480p, $0.10/s at 720p, $0.20/s at 1080p (30% discount active through September 24). Chinese data routing applies — files uploaded to the API pass through Alibaba infrastructure.

Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/alibaba-launches-wan30-video-generation-model-2233eb30), [TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2026/08/10/chinas-alibaba-releases-wan3-0-ai-video-model-in-public-beta-with-30s-clips-multimodal-inputs/), [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/325426/20260825/alibaba-wan30-turns-business-documents-video-your-files-route-through-chinese-law.htm)
