## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Google TPU 8tx and 8i Max: Confidential Computing and Large-Memory Inference

Google announced two additions to its TPU 8 family on November 9. The **TPU 8tx** is Google Cloud's first TPU with confidential computing — data stays encrypted during inference using AMD EPYC 5th-gen host CPUs, enabling secure-enclave-style workloads. The **TPU 8i Max** combines two TPU 8i dies on one package, targeting long-context inference that needs more HBM. Neither is new silicon; both extend existing TPU 8 designs. Google's AI chip business is approaching 100 Cloud TPU customers. With TPU 8t and 8i from April 2026, that's four TPU systems in one year.

Sources: [Data Center Dynamics](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-expands-eighth-generation-tpu-portfolio-with-two-new-tpu-systems), [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/google-unveils-tpu-8tx-and-8i-max-for-secure-long-context-ai), [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/google-tpu-8tx-8i-max-confidential-computing-amd-epyc/)

**Why it matters:** The 8tx is the first GPU/TPU-class accelerator with in-use encryption on Google Cloud — directly relevant for regulated workloads (healthcare, finance, gov) where model weights or patient data can't leave the enclave. Clear signal that enterprise AI security requirements are shaping chip roadmaps.
