## Anthropic

### [NEW] Novo Nordisk partners with Anthropic to accelerate drug discovery

Novo Nordisk and Anthropic announced on September 16 a named collaboration to deploy Claude in Novo's R&D organization. Novo will use frontier Claude models plus the **Claude Science Workbench** — Anthropic's specialized research environment — in targeted scientific workflows covering biological reasoning, drug discovery, and clinical development. The partnership also extends to AI-driven software development inside Novo as a scaling vector. This is distinct from Anthropic's LSVP verification program (which opened broadly in September); Novo is a named premier partner with jointly designed workflows. Sources: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/16/3363106/0/en/novo-and-anthropic-will-collaborate-to-advance-drug-discovery-with-claude), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/novo-will-work-with-anthropic-ai-to-speed-drug-development), [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/novo-and-anthropic-will-collaborate-to-advance-drug-discovery-with-claude).

**Why it matters:** Novo is the largest pharma company by market cap in Europe. This is concrete pipeline evidence for Anthropic's life-sciences revenue track ahead of the IPO.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Grok 4.7 — 2.1T parameters, same price, SpaceX training data

xAI released Grok 4.7 on September 21, ending a series of delays that stretched from late July. Key specs: **2.1 trillion parameters** (up ~40% from Grok 4.6's 1.5T), $2/$6 per million input/output tokens (unchanged), **500K-token context window**. xAI used a longer RL training run and weighted more heavily toward difficult multi-hour tasks; the model self-verifies answers more aggressively than 4.6. Unusually, xAI folded in supplemental training data from SpaceX: Starlink satellite telemetry, manufacturing records, and engineering failure logs — positioning Grok 4.7 as stronger at hardware/physical-systems reasoning. Available day-one in the Grok app, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Grok Build, and xAI API. Sources: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/378824/xai-launches-grok-4-7), [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/blog/research/grok-4-7-launch), [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-21-grok-4-7-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/).

**Why it matters:** The SpaceX training-data angle is a real architectural differentiator, not just a benchmark bump. At the same price as 4.6 and with Copilot integration day-one, it's an immediate consideration for any eval stack.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] US military AI hallucination nearly triggered armed clash with China

CNN published an exclusive on September 18: earlier in 2026, during the Iran conflict, a US Special Operations Command analyst asked an AI chatbot to review manifest intelligence on a Chinese vessel in the Middle East. The chatbot **hallucinated nuclear weapon components** aboard the ship. The false report circulated across the military chain. Armed boarding teams were staged; aircraft were in the air en route to intercept the vessel. Officials caught the error just before the operation executed. Sources called the incident "almost started a war." No AI vendor was named in the report. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/us-military-ai-false-intelligence-china-ship), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2263043/ai-almost-led-the-us-military-to-attack-china-report-says/), [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/world/us/ai-almost-started-a-war-between-us-china-4151738).

**Why it matters:** This is the highest-stakes publicly confirmed AI hallucination incident to date. The timing, one week before the Trump-Xi summit (Sept 24) with AI safety on the agenda, makes it politically immediate. Expect this to drive near-term military AI procurement policy changes.
