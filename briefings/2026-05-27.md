## Model Releases

### [NEW] Gemini Omni: any-modality-to-video generation, rolling out now

Google launched Gemini Omni at I/O on May 19 and it is now rolling out to all Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers globally via the Gemini app and Google Flow. Omni accepts any combination of text, image, audio, and existing video as input and generates physics-aware, conversationally editable video clips (~10 seconds), with synchronized audio generated from scratch. Each instruction round maintains character, lighting, and object consistency. Omni Flash is also live at no cost inside YouTube Shorts Remix and the YouTube Create app for users 18+; voice-cloning from existing video audio is explicitly blocked. Gemini Omni is distinct from Gemini 3.5 Flash (the coding/agent model GA'd at I/O) — Omni is a dedicated video-generation model.

**Why it matters:** Google's video generation is now chat-driven and integrated into the same subscription developers already use for Gemini API access, which lowers the friction for building video-output agents.

Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-gemini-omni-ai-video-generation/), [BusinessWorld](https://www.bworldonline.com/technology/2026/05/20/751019/google-launches-gemini-omni-capable-of-chat-based-video-generation-editing/)

### [NEW] Stable Audio 3.0: six-minute open-weight music generation on fully licensed data

Stability AI shipped Stable Audio 3.0 on May 20: four models at 459M, 459M, 1.4B, and 2.7B parameters. Small and SFX models handle up to 2 minutes on-device; medium (1.4B) and large (2.7B) generate up to 6:20 of full compositions maintaining melodic and structural coherence — more than double Stable Audio 2.0's capacity. Small SFX, small, and medium are open-weight; large is API-only. Training data is fully licensed (WMG and UMG deals signed 2025). Ethan Kaplan (former CDO at Universal Audio and Fender) joined Stability to lead the professional music offering.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/stability-ai-release-a-new-audio-model-that-can-create-six-minute-songs/), [Stability AI](https://stability.ai/stable-audio), [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/05/21/stability-ai-launches-its-latest-fully-licensed-music-model/)

### [NEW] Grok 4.3: xAI's cost-efficient agentic flagship (May 4 catchup)

Grok 4.3 launched May 4 as xAI's current cost-efficient frontier model: built-in reasoning, 1M-token context, native video input, priced at $1.25/M input tokens. Scores 53 on the Intelligence Index (median: 35), ranks #1 on CaseLaw v2 and CorpFin benchmarks. Three weeks on, it has reached GA on Microsoft Foundry and OCI Generative AI. On May 22, Grok expanded third-party connectors to Vercel, Canva, Gamma, and S&P Global. Legacy Grok 4.20 and eight prior models were retired from the API on May 15.

Sources: [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/xai-launches-grok-4-3-with-improved-agentic-performance-and-lower-pricing), [Apiyi](https://help.apiyi.com/en/grok-4-3-api-release-may-2026-news-en.html)

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] ChatGPT for Excel and Google Sheets: out of beta, globally available

OpenAI's spreadsheet sidebar — which lets you build, update, and debug multi-tab Excel and Google Sheets files in natural language — has exited beta and is now available globally to Business, Enterprise, Edu, K-12, and ChatGPT Free/Go/Plus/Pro users (EU plans: outside EU only). Business/Enterprise/Edu have a free preview through June 2, after which usage follows plan credits. Excel beta launched March 5; Sheets joined April 22; GA confirmed late May. Financial data integrations (FactSet, Bloomberg, S&P data via ChatGPT Pro) ship alongside.

**Why it matters:** This pairs with the existing OpenAI Codex mobile/web agent story — OpenAI is building a consistent "AI-in-every-productivity-surface" surface, and the spreadsheet integration specifically hits the quantitative workflow Stack runs (trading systems, data pipelines).

Sources: [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-excel/), [Digital Trends GA confirmation](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/chatgpt-for-excel-and-google-sheets-is-now-out-of-beta-and-its-kind-of-a-big-deal/)
