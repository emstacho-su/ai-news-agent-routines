## Research & Technical

### [NEW] SubQ 1M-Preview: first fully subquadratic LLM, 12M-token native context, 52× faster attention at 1M tokens

Subquadratic — an 11-PhD Miami startup — emerged from stealth May 5 with $29M raised and SubQ 1M-Preview: the first LLM built on a fully subquadratic sparse attention architecture where both compute and memory scale linearly with context length (not O(n²)). The architecture is called Subquadratic Selective Attention (SSA). Key numbers: 52× faster than FlashAttention at 1M tokens; approximately 1,000× lower attention compute at 12M tokens vs a dense baseline; 92.1% needle-in-a-haystack at 12M tokens (no transformer model operates at this length). Standard benchmarks: 82.4% SWE-bench Verified (edges Opus 4.6's 81.4%), RULER@128K 95.0% vs Claude Opus 4.6's 94.8%. Pricing claims approximately one-fifth the cost of frontier models at equivalent context lengths. [Subquadratic intro](https://subq.ai/introducing-subq) | [DataCamp explainer](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/subq-ai-explained) | [Hacker News thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48023079) | [SiliconANGLE on funding](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/05/subquadratic-launches-29m-bring-12m-token-context-windows-ai/)

**Why it matters:** Transformer O(n²) attention is the primary practical barrier to genuine long-context reasoning at scale. If SSA's linear scaling holds under production load and adversarial inputs, this is the most significant architecture departure from the standard transformer stack to reach commercial availability. The HN thread is tracking independent benchmark replication — worth following before building on this.
