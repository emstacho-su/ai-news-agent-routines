## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] S-1/A adds Q3 results: $16.96B quarterly revenue, roadshow week of Nov 9

**Background:** Anthropic filed its public S-1 on Oct 26, disclosing $16.3B in H1 2026 revenue, $80.7B H1 net loss, Nasdaq ticker ANT, and a roadshow target of week of Nov 9.

**New:** The Nov 2 amended S-1/A adds Q3: revenue reached $16.96B (from $11.56B in Q2), bringing nine-month revenue to $33.24B. Customers billed ≥$100K ARR year-over-year jumped from ~950 to 11,900 — the enterprise adoption curve is steep. WSJ reports institutional investors are pressing the valuation target down to $1.5–1.6T from the $1.8–2T target floated at the Oct 14 investor day. Sequential revenue growth rate decelerated to ~47% in Q3 (from 144% in Q2), which is the main concern ahead of the roadshow.

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] DeepSeek bets Huawei Ascend on 2T-parameter model pre-training

DeepSeek plans to pre-train a 2T-parameter model on 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT chips at a facility in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, by end of 2026. CEO Liang Wenfeng called the domestic-chip strategy "one of the company's biggest strategic bets." Context: an earlier attempt to pre-train on Ascend hardware reportedly failed in 2025 due to unstable performance and immature software stack; Huawei engineers have since moved into DeepSeek's offices. Huawei's next-gen 960DT is expected Q1 2027, with DeepSeek getting priority allocation. The company is also targeting an eventual 8T-parameter run.

**Why it matters:** If the 2T run succeeds, it validates the Ascend stack at frontier scale and breaks the structural Nvidia dependency for Chinese labs. Stack: watch whether Epoch AI's compute estimates for Ascend vs. Nvidia in a full pre-training run start to narrow — this is the benchmark to follow.

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] AI datacenter ballot measures: election delivers across-the-board defeat

**Background:** 39–49 local ballot measures on AI data center construction appeared on Nov 3 2026 midterm ballots across 8–10 states; all 6 measures decided before Election Day went against development.

**New (unofficial results):** Central Ohio voters rejected datacenters in every local ballot issue. Urbana, OH passed a data center ban with ~77% of the vote. Democratic candidates who pledged to halt data center development claimed victory in key races. Euronews reports the anti-datacenter slate performed well broadly. Elon Musk responded by suggesting space-based data centers now look "appealing." Fast Company's pre-election coverage had 84% of November measures resolved on Election Day.

### [RUMOR] Oracle scraps $7B Tencent chip lease after US pressure

A single report (implicator.ai, citing sources) says Oracle dropped a five-year, 100,000-chip lease with Tencent that had been structured to route advanced AI chips through Southeast Asian data centers outside mainland China's export-control perimeter. Neither Oracle nor Tencent has confirmed the cancellation. Prior analysis found the arrangement was likely legal under current export law; a Senate bill (S. 3519) that would have added remote compute access as a regulable category stalled in committee. Treat as unconfirmed until a second source or company statement emerges.
