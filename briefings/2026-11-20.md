## Anthropic

## Model Releases

### [UPDATE] Google Gemini 4 Neon: codename → GA in 48 hours

**Background:** A "Gemini 4 Neon" string was spotted in app source code on Nov 16, flagged as a likely mid-tier release between Flash-Lite and a full Flash tier.

**New:** Google officially released Gemini 4 Neon on Nov 18, 2026. It's now available in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini Antigravity (paid subscribers), and rolling out to Gemini Pro/Ultra users in the app. Pricing: $3.00/M input, $15.00/M output. Context window: 1,048,576 tokens; max output: 65,536 tokens. Google says it reduces repeated file reads in long tool-use sessions and can debug across repositories with fewer round-trips — directly relevant for multi-step coding agents.

Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/), [Vercel AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/changelog/gemini-4-neon-now-available-on-ai-gateway)

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Palo Alto Networks acquires Applied Compute for $4.5B

Palo Alto Networks announced an all-stock deal to acquire Applied Compute, a startup that provides post-training and inference infrastructure for open-weight AI models. Applied Compute had $100M+ ARR (customers include Microsoft and DoorDash) and raised $500M+ total. Applied Compute's co-founder and CEO Yash Patil joins PANW as co-chief AI officer. CEO Nikesh Arora framed this as "every enterprise will run a mix of open and closed models, and every one needs to be secured." Deal closes PANW fiscal Q3 pending regulatory clearance.

**Why it matters:** Signals that the enterprise security play for open-weight models is real — not just hosting, but hardening fine-tuned checkpoints before prod deployment. If you're building agents with open-weight backends, this is the direction enterprise procurement is heading.

Sources: [AIWeekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/palo-alto-networks-to-buy-ai-startup-applied-compute-for-45b-weeks-after-its), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001327567/000132756726000030/ex992pressrelease_november.htm), [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/palo-alto-networks-acquires-applied-compute-45-billion)

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] Nvidia confirms halt of all licensed Hopper China shipments (earnings call, Nov 18)

**Background:** ~600K Nvidia H200 chips have been stockpiling in Hong Kong since Beijing declined to approve large-scale imports into mainland China.

**New:** On the Nov 18 FY2027 Q3 earnings call, interim CEO Colette Kress stated that Nvidia has stopped shipping all licensed Hopper-generation chips to China — even those previously approved under US export licenses. Future guidance excludes all licensed Hopper products bound for Chinese customers. This is a qualitative escalation: it's no longer just Beijing blocking imports; Nvidia itself has stopped the pipeline.

Sources: [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/fox-news-tech/nvidia-halts-china-ai-chip-shipments-despite-us-approval.amp), [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-chips-export-controls-china), [BDNews24](https://bdnews24.com/business/pyentjtvr9)

### [NEW] White House accuses Moonshot AI of training Kimi K3 on banned Nvidia chips via Thailand

A White House official cited by Bloomberg alleged that Moonshot AI rented 32 Super Micro Computer servers each equipped with Nvidia B300 GPUs hosted in Thailand to train Kimi K3, circumventing US export controls. This follows earlier allegations in July 2026 that Moonshot distilled Fable 5 (the Treasury sanctions-threat story). Moonshot has not publicly confirmed or denied the specific chip allegations. The KuCoin and Daily Caller reports characterize it as an ongoing investigation, not a sanctions action yet.

Sources: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/white-house-official-says-moonshot-accessed-banned-nvidia-chips), [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/white-house-accuses-moonshot-ai-of-using-us-banned-nvidia-chips-via-thailand-servers)
