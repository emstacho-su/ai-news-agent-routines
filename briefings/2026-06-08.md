## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Anthropic files confidential S-1 at $965B valuation; $47B ARR, October IPO target

**Background:** We've tracked Anthropic's funding arc from ~$850B preemptive offers through the ~$900B range; Q2 profitability projections were first surfaced in May.

**New:** Anthropic closed a $65B Series H on May 28 and filed a confidential draft S-1 with the SEC on June 1, 2026. Post-money valuation is $965B — ahead of OpenAI's $852B March figure. Revenue run-rate hit $47B in May (up from ~$10B a year ago). Q2 2026 projections: $10.9B revenue, ~$559M operating profit (~5% margin). IPO target is October 2026, part of what analysts are calling a three-way simultaneous public-market race with OpenAI and SpaceX. No share count or price range has been set; the S-1 is under SEC confidential review.

Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-confidentially-files-ipo-965-billion-valuation/), [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec), [FX Leaders](https://www.fxleaders.com/news/2026/06/02/anthropic-files-for-ipo-at-965-billion-valuation-could-become-the-defining-ai-listing-of-the-decade/)

**Why it matters:** The 5% operating margin is the number to watch — it validates running on bundled-quota infrastructure (Routines, managed agents) rather than burning raw API tokens. If Anthropic can hold this margin at IPO scale, it reframes the "AI is a money-losing utility" narrative.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Apple WWDC 2026: Gemini-powered Siri confirmed; iOS 27 Extensions system makes Claude an iPhone-native AI option

**Background:** Apple announced a Gemini licensing partnership in January 2026; WWDC was scheduled for June 8–12 with Siri and iOS 27 as the main AI storyline.

**New:** Today's keynote confirmed the full architecture. Siri's cloud functions are now powered by a ~1.2-trillion-parameter custom Google Gemini model licensed at approximately $1B/year. Separately, iOS 27 ships an **Extensions** system: install a compatible AI app from the App Store, enable its Extension in Settings, and select Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT as the backend for Siri queries, Writing Tools, and Image Playground. Default Siri remains Gemini-powered regardless of the active Extension. iOS 27 Beta 1 shipped this afternoon; consumer rollout is fall 2026.

Sources: [NerdLevelTech](https://nerdleveltech.com/ios-27-extensions-third-party-ai-models-apple-intelligence), [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/wwdc-2026-what-to-expect/), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/apple-wwdc-2026-siri-ai-overhaul/)

**Why it matters:** The Extensions framework creates a new distribution surface for Claude-powered apps on iOS. Developer docs ship with Beta 1 — if Extensions exposes a programmable API rather than just a settings toggle, it could become an interesting ingress path for agent workflows running inside Claude Code.

### [UPDATE] Claude Opus 4.8 added to Microsoft Foundry 11,000-model catalog

**Background:** Claude Opus 4.8 launched May 28 with Dynamic Workflows research preview; Foundry has been a distribution channel for third-party Claude models.

**New:** Claude Opus 4.8 is now in the Microsoft Foundry model catalog alongside what Microsoft is calling its finalized 11,000-model roster. Specs on Foundry: 200K context window, $5/1M input tokens, $25/1M output tokens. One gotcha: standard Azure credits do not apply to these catalog models — a separately configured Foundry billing subscription is required.

Sources: [Azure Community Hub](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azure-ai-foundry-blog/claude-opus-4-8-is-now-available-in-microsoft-foundry/4523367), [BuildFastWithAI June 8](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-8-2026)

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Pentagon tests OpenAI, Google, and xAI to replace Anthropic; OpenAI and Gemini already live in some DoD systems

In March 2026, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth designated Anthropic's products a supply-chain risk after Anthropic refused to remove safety guardrails that bar its AI from mass-surveillance and lethal-autonomous-weapons applications. The DoD issued a 180-day deadline to phase Claude out of all military workflows (expires approximately September 2026) and required defense vendors to certify non-use of Claude in DoD contracts. Competitive testing of OpenAI, Google Gemini, and xAI Grok kicked off March 1. As of early June, OpenAI models and Gemini are reportedly live in several DoD systems. The Pentagon's CTO said he is "pretty confident" the transition can complete on schedule; security experts warn that each classified integration must be individually offboarded and re-cleared.

Sources: [Bloomberg May 21](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/pentagon-tests-rival-ai-models-in-race-to-replace-anthropic), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/09/anthropic-was-the-pentagons-choice-for-ai-now-its-banned-and-experts-are-worried.html), [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/defense-news/2026/03/dod-confident-it-can-replace-anthropics-claude-within-six-months-but-some-warn-transition-wont-be-easy/), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/threats/2026/02/it-would-take-pentagon-months-replace-anthropics-ai-tools-sources/411741/)

**Why it matters:** DoD contracts are high-trust anchors that pull in consulting and systems-integration spend. Losing them reinforces why Anthropic has been building alternative enterprise revenue channels (Goldman JV, KPMG, Gates Foundation) at such velocity. Also relevant context for Stack's security concentration — the dispute turns on exactly where AI safety restrictions should and should not apply.

### [NEW] EU AI Act: 55 days to full enforcement; Code of Practice on AI content labeling due this month

August 2, 2026 is the EU AI Act's primary enforcement date — the day the full regulation applies to all in-scope systems, including general-purpose AI (GPAI) model providers. The June 2026 Code of Practice on marking and labeling AI-generated content is scheduled for publication this month, giving generative AI providers standardized transparency obligations to implement. Note: the European Parliament voted to delay high-risk AI system compliance from August 2026 to December 2027, but that delay requires EU Council agreement before the August 2 deadline — the Council has not yet acted. US companies serving EU-resident users face the August 2 deadline regardless of the Parliament vote's outcome.

Sources: [EU Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/us-companies-face-eu-ai-acts-possible-august-2026-compliance-deadline), [A-LIGN](https://www.a-lign.com/articles/eu-ai-act-enforcement-delay)
