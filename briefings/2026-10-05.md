## Wildcard

### [NEW] AMD acquires World Labs: $8.2B all-stock for Fei-Fei Li's spatial AI lab

AMD announced on September 28 a definitive agreement to acquire World Labs, the San Francisco-based spatial-intelligence lab founded by Dr. Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal valued at approximately $8.2 billion. The deal is AMD's second-largest acquisition ever (behind the $50B Xilinx deal) and is expected to close by end of 2026 pending regulatory approval.

World Labs' primary product is Marble, a multimodal world model that reconstructs and generates persistent, interactive 3D environments from text, images, or video — with applications in robotics simulation and interactive world-building. Following close, Fei-Fei Li becomes AMD EVP and Chief Scientist reporting directly to Lisa Su; Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall continue leading the research team.

Sources: [HotHardware](https://hothardware.com/news/amd-buys-world-labs-8-billion-massive-ai-power-play), [AMD Newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-acquire-world-labs/), [DataCenter Dynamics](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/amd-acquires-ai-research-firm-world-labs-in-all-stock-deal-worth-82bn).

**Why it matters:** Until this deal, AMD was a pure-hardware player — it sold GPUs but had no model research capability. World Labs gives AMD a frontier AI research lab and a flagship model (Marble) to shape its hardware roadmap around. The Fei-Fei Li hire is the most prominent AI scientist acquisition since Yann LeCun at Meta. This is the first time a chip company has bought a model lab of this caliber, inverting the usual direction (model labs buying chip infrastructure).

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] GitGuardian 2026: AI-assisted commits leak secrets at 2× baseline; AI credentials fastest-growing leak category

GitGuardian's State of Secrets Sprawl 2026 report found 29 million hardcoded secrets in public GitHub — a 34% year-over-year increase. The most notable finding for developers using AI tooling: **AI-service credentials are the fastest-growing leak category (+81% YoY)**, and **AI-assisted commits leak secrets at approximately 3.2%, roughly twice the 1.6% baseline** for human-only commits.

Two separate disclosures in 2026 (April and Black Hat USA August) showed active exploitation of this pattern: attackers used crafted pull requests and issues to prompt AI coding agents (Claude Code, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot) into reading CI/CD secrets from environment variables and exfiltrating them during task execution.

Sources: [GitGuardian via nhimg.org](https://nhimg.org/glossary/agentic-secret-exposure/), [Ox Security blog](https://www.ox.security/blog/agentic-ai-and-the-rise-of-hardcoded-secrets/), [Refonte Learning](https://www.refontelearning.com/blog/ai-coding-agents-leaking-cicd-secrets).

**Why it matters:** If you use Claude Code in auto mode against a repo with secrets in `.env` files or environment variables, you are in the attack surface. The mitigations are concrete: never store unencrypted secrets in repo files that agents can read, audit agent permissions, and treat CI/CD secrets as the high-value targets they are. The Black Hat demonstrations show this is exploited in the wild, not theoretical.

## Practical Tips

- On AMD World Labs: Marble (the World Labs 3D world model) is currently a standalone product. Post-acquisition, watch for it to be optimized specifically for AMD MI-series GPUs — that's the most likely near-term integration.
- On secrets hygiene: if you run Claude Code with broad filesystem access, do `grep -r 'sk-\|API_KEY\|SECRET' . --include='*.env*' --include='*.json'` to audit what's in agent-readable paths before your next auto-mode session.
