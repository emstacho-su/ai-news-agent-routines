## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Apple WWDC 2026 (June 8-12): rebuilt Siri, iOS 27 Extensions let users choose Claude or ChatGPT as system agent
Apple's WWDC keynote lands June 8 at 10 AM PT, with iOS 27 as the centerpiece. The big AI story: Siri is getting a complete ground-up rebuild powered by a custom 1.2T-parameter Gemini model (Apple-Google deal, announced January 2026, ~$1B/year; all inference runs through Apple's Private Cloud Compute — end-to-end encrypted, no data retained at Google). The developer-relevant angle: iOS 27 introduces an **Extensions** framework in the Siri surface that lets users select third-party AI agents as their default system-level intelligence — Claude and ChatGPT are both explicitly named as options. This is the first time Apple has opened the Siri invocation layer to competing AI agents. Also shipping: a standalone Siri app with conversation history, a new system-wide "Search or Ask" gesture, Visual Intelligence upgrades (reads nutrition labels, extracts business-card contacts), and a new `genai.apple.com` landing page. [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/wwdc-2026), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/12/ios-27s-completely-rebuilt-siri-will-include-a-new-system-wide-search-gesture-report/), [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/apple-hints-at-major-ai-announcement-as-new-gen-ai-page-appears-ahead-of-wwdc-2026-533078-2026-05-25)

**Why it matters:** If the Extensions framework ships as described, Claude becomes a system-agent option for ~1B active iPhone users. Worth watching the developer session coverage on June 8 for API details.

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] Pope Leo XIV "Magnifica Humanitas" — full text out today; bans autonomous weapons, warns AI fuels inequality and warfare
**Background:** Encyclical titled "Magnifica Humanitas: On the Protection of Human Dignity in the Age of Artificial Intelligence," signed May 15 (135th anniversary of *Rerum novarum*); Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah was invited to speak at the Vatican launch event.
**New:** Full text released today (May 25, 11:30 AM at Vatican Synod Hall), with Pope Leo XIV personally presenting — unusual for encyclical launches. Key positions from the actual document: explicit call for a ban on lethal autonomous weapons systems without human oversight; warning that AI is being deployed to "fuel warfare"; characterizes the core challenge as "anthropological, not technological"; calls for AI developers to adopt "shared standards of social justice"; directly criticizes Big Tech concentration of AI capabilities as widening inequality, weakening democracy, and "undermining what it means to be human."
Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/25/europe/pope-leo-ai-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas-intl), [Religion News Service](https://religionnews.com/2026/05/25/in-his-first-encyclical-pope-leo-xiv-says-ai-must-serve-humanity-not-the-powerful-few/), [National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/cna/pope-leo-ai-magnifica-humanitas), [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-05/pope-leo-xiv-first-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas.html)

### [NEW] NextEra Energy buys Dominion for $67B to power AI data centers — largest US utility merger
NextEra Energy is acquiring Dominion Energy in a $67B all-stock deal (announced May 18) to create the world's largest regulated utility, with one goal explicitly driving the deal: meeting electricity demand from hyperscale AI data centers. Dominion is the critical asset — it controls the power grid across Northern Virginia, home to the world's highest concentration of data centers operated by Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Meta. AI training loads have pushed Virginia grid demand to record levels, creating an infrastructure chokepoint. Combined entity: $249B market cap, $420B enterprise value, 130 GW construction backlog (exceeding existing total generation capacity). Regulatory approval expected to take until mid-to-late 2027. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/18/nextera-nee-dominion-energy-d-data-center-ai.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/18/nextera-dominion-67-billion-acquisition-ai-data-centers-largest-utility/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/05/21/ai-data-centers-are-about-to-break-the-grid-one-company-just-spent-67-billion-to-fix-it/)
