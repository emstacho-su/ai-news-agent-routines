## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] IPO valuation expectations slide to ~$1.5T after Q3 revenue miss

**Background:** Anthropic held a pre-IPO investor day at its SF HQ on Oct 14, targeting a $1.8–2T Nasdaq listing before Thanksgiving. Roadshow was expected to begin the week of Nov 9.

**New:** Reuters reported Oct 15–17, citing people familiar with the matter, that investors who attended the investor day are now discussing a valuation of approximately $1.5 trillion — roughly 25% below the $2T that had been the working consensus. The reset follows the Q3 2026 revenue disclosure: $16.8B, +45% QoQ from Q2's $11.6B. The growth rate, while still strong in absolute terms, was slower sequentially than investors had modeled. Anthropic said it remains profitable on an adjusted basis. Even at $1.5T, the listing would be the largest US IPO in history (SpaceX listed at $1.77T in June).

## Wildcard

### [NEW] California AI executive order: independent verification + kill switch mandate

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order on September 18, 2026 directing state agencies to advance two requirements for frontier AI models: (1) independent verification by an external organization confirming safety properties, and (2) a "kill switch" mechanism for rogue AI agents. The EO accelerates implementation of SB 813 (independent auditor registries) and AB 1405 (state auditor oversight). This week, his office named Gillian Hadfield (University of Toronto), Alondra Nelson (IAS Princeton), and Rob Reich (Stanford) as expert advisors to develop the kill switch proposal; their recommendations are due November 15–16. The order follows an incident in which 700 AI agents attacked Hugging Face infrastructure during an OpenAI cybersecurity experiment in July.

Critics from CAIDP argue the kill-switch provision is weaker than ex ante termination obligations in SB 1047, the bill Newsom vetoed in 2024. Multiple outlets link the policy shift to his likely 2028 presidential positioning.

**Why it matters:** Independent third-party verification requirements — if they make it into law — would directly affect Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and any org deploying frontier models in California. Watch the Nov 15 deadline.

Sources: [Gov.ca.gov](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/23/governor-newsom-announces-world-leading-experts-to-deliver-on-his-ai-executive-order-including-advancing-creation-of-a-kill-switch/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/17/california-gov-newsom-boosts-ai-regulation-as-he-eyes-2028-presidential-run), [Daily Bruin](https://dailybruin.com/2026/10/09/gavin-newsoms-executive-order-increases-ai-oversight-proposes-kill-switch), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/newsom-advances-ai-kill-switch-mandate-under-new-california-executive-order)

### [NEW] AI-generated midterm ads: $137M spent, ~70% without disclosure

Wesleyan Media Project data, reported by the New York Times, puts total 2026 US midterm spending on AI-generated or AI-enhanced political ads at $137M across roughly 220 identified ads — compared to under $50K in the entire 2024 cycle. Roughly 70–80% of the ads carry no AI disclosure, and about 80% come from Republican campaigns or GOP-aligned PACs. Wesleyan researchers note nearly half of identified ads depict real politicians in fabricated scenarios. Thirty-one states have AI-disclosure laws for political ads; no federal rule exists ahead of the November 3 vote.

**Why it matters:** Scale of adoption is faster than regulatory coverage. This is the benchmark data for the first major election cycle where generative AI advertising was deployed at scale.

Sources: [Gizmodo / NYT](https://gizmodo.com/137-million-has-been-spent-on-ai-generated-ads-ahead-of-midterms-only-27-included-a-disclaimer-2000827138), [AI Weekly / Wesleyan](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/nyt-wesleyan-finds-137m-spent-on-220-ai-generated-midterm-ads-70-carry-no)
