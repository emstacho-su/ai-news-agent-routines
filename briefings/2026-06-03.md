## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Project Glasswing expands to ~200 orgs in 15+ countries (June 2–3)

**Background:** Project Glasswing launched in April 2026 with ~40 organizations (AWS, Apple, Microsoft, Google, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks) receiving restricted access to Claude Mythos Preview for autonomous vulnerability scanning.

**New:** Anthropic added ~150 more organizations, bringing the total to roughly 200 across 15+ countries. The new cohort adds sectors absent from the first wave: power grids, water systems, healthcare providers, communications networks, and hardware manufacturers. Initial Glasswing partners have already surfaced more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity security flaws. Claude Security (codebase scanning and patch suggestions) is bundled into the expanded program. Anthropic says further expansion will prioritize critical open-source software maintainers and safety testers.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-mythos-ai-project-glasswing.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing), [HelpNetSecurity](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/03/anthropic-project-glasswing-expansion/)

**Why it matters:** If you're building on infrastructure that overlaps with any of the expanded sectors, Glasswing partner access is now worth requesting — the vulnerability surface covered is broadening fast.

### Claude global outage — June 2, 2026

A widespread outage hit claude.ai, the API, Console, and Claude Code from approximately 06:04 UTC to 11:49 UTC on June 2 (~5.75 hours). Root cause: a bug in the sub-agent orchestration system caused agents to multiply exponentially in an infinite loop, exhausting capacity. Many users reported their multi-hour or multi-day usage allowances were fully drained in minutes during the incident. Anthropic confirmed "capacity constraints" and said service is fully restored.

The timing was noted: the outage began the morning after Anthropic filed its confidential IPO S-1 with the SEC.

Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/claude-celebrates-anthropics-stock-market-float-with-blockbuster-outage/5250071), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/live/claude-outage-june-2026), [The National News](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/06/02/anthropics-claude-hit-by-major-global-outage-due-to-unexpected-capacity-constraints/)

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Microsoft Build 2026 Day 2: Aion 1.0 on-device Windows AI models

**Background:** Build 2026 keynote (June 2) shipped Project Polaris MoE coding model for GitHub Copilot, MAI model family (Transcribe-1, Voice-1, Image-2, Thinking-1), Copilot Workspace GA, and Windows Agent Framework v1.0.

**New (Day 2):** Microsoft revealed **Aion 1.0** — two on-device models shipping directly inside Windows on capable hardware:

- **Aion 1.0 Plan**: 14B parameter reasoning and tool-calling model, 32K context, designed for local agentic workflows (file orchestration, sub-agent spawning, intent reasoning) with no cloud dependency. Ships in-box with Windows; GA timeline "coming months."
- **Aion 1.0 Instruct**: Smaller SLM for everyday text intelligence (summarization, rewrite, accessibility). Open weights releasing on Hugging Face in July 2026; available in Edge Insider today.

First time Microsoft has shipped an on-device reasoning/agent model as a Windows OS component rather than an optional download.

Sources: [WinCentral](https://thewincentral.com/microsoft-aion-1-ai-models-expanded-windows-ai-apis-build-2026/), [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/microsoft-unveils-aion-10-on-device-ai-at-build-2026), [Windows Developer Blog](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2026/06/02/build-2026-furthering-windows-as-the-trusted-platform-for-development/), [ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/microsoft-build-2026-windows-ai-models-aion-local-inference-builder-guide/)

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] Trump signs AI executive order with voluntary 30-day model sharing (June 2)

**Background:** A 90-day pre-release model sharing EO was negotiated with OpenAI, Anthropic, and other labs in May 2026 but canceled at the last minute after Musk, Zuckerberg, and David Sacks each called Trump directly to oppose it. Trump said he "didn't like certain aspects of it."

**New:** A revised EO was signed June 2, 2026. Key provisions:

1. **Voluntary 30-day review**: AI companies may voluntarily give the federal government up to 30 days of pre-release access to frontier models for national security evaluation. Explicitly non-mandatory; cannot be used to create a licensing or preclearance requirement.
2. **AI cybersecurity clearinghouse**: Treasury, NSA, CISA, and NIST to form a clearinghouse within 30 days to coordinate vulnerability scanning, validation, and patch distribution across AI industry and critical infrastructure operators.
3. **Benchmark process**: 60-day window to develop criteria for what constitutes a "covered frontier model" focused on advanced cyber capabilities.

TechTimes confirms Musk and Zuckerberg successfully lobbied the review window down from 90 to 30 days before this version was signed.

Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/02/tech/executive-order-trump-ai), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/trump-signs-executive-order-that-allows-voluntary-federal-vetting-of-top-ai-models-for-national-security-risks), [WhiteHouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317626/20260602/trump-ai-executive-order-signed-musk-zuckerberg-lobbied-review-down-30-days.htm)

### [NEW] Microsoft Majorana 2 quantum chip — 1,000× qubit reliability, 2029 fault-tolerant target

Microsoft unveiled Majorana 2 at Build 2026, claiming a 1,000-fold improvement in qubit reliability over previous generations. Key claims: average qubit lifetimes extend to ~20 seconds (some up to one minute) versus milliseconds previously, achieved by replacing aluminum superconductors with lead. Microsoft developed the chip with assistance from its Microsoft Discovery agentic AI research platform. Roadmap targets a fault-tolerant system by 2029.

Standard caveat: topological quantum computing has a history of retracted papers and skeptical physicists — DigiTimes and Science News both flag expert skepticism about the claims.

Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-02/microsoft-s-new-quantum-chip-aiming-for-useful-machine-in-2029), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317648/20260602/majorana-2-quantum-chip-revealed-microsoft-build-2026-features-specs-explained.htm), [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/02/microsofts-new-majorana-2-quantum-chip-claims-dramatic-breakthrough-qubit-stability/)
