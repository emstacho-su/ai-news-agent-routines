## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] IPO roadshow starts, valuation compresses further to $1–1.3T

**Background:** Anthropic filed its S-1/A on Nov 2, disclosing Q3 2026 revenue of ~$17B and first operating-profitable quarter ($974M op. profit). The formal roadshow was flagged for the week of Nov 9 with earliest trading est. Nov 16.

**New:** Roadshow marketing kicked off today. Amid Friday's AI-sector selloff (Nasdaq -5% week-over-week, Nvidia -5% today alone), Bloomberg pegs current investor conversations at $1.2–1.3T. ## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Goldman Sachs restricts Claude Code and GitHub Copilot to engineering teams only

Goldman Sachs has limited access to Claude Code and GitHub Copilot to its engineering organization, blocking broader firm-wide rollout. The restriction was flagged in several daily AI digests today alongside the broader AI market selloff narrative.

This follows Microsoft's enterprise license cancellation (tracked July 2026) as another data point in the enterprise pattern: large financial institutions are deploying AI coding tools inside engineering perimeters only, not firm-wide. Goldman is simultaneously an investor in Ode with Anthropic (the Anthropic–Goldman–Blackstone AI-services JV) and an enterprise Claude customer — the internal restriction is a risk-management play, not a repudiation of the relationship.

Sources: [The Neuron (Nov 9 digest)](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-9-2026/), [DEV Community weekly digest](https://dev.to/gksu_demirci_56f752953e7/weekly-ai-digest-november-9-2026-p6a)

**Why it matters:** If you're designing multi-tenant MCP deployments or building tooling for financial-sector orgs, this is the emerging compliance posture: coding AI = engineering-gated. Expect IT policy templates to follow.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Nvidia board opens independent probe into Jensen Huang's alleged affair with Chinese intelligence asset

Nvidia's board has launched an independent investigation — led by a committee of independent directors with outside counsel — after CEO Jensen Huang admitted to an affair with a businesswoman identified by U.S. intelligence as allegedly recruited by China's top intelligence agency. The woman, Alice Huang, is affiliated with Megaspeed, a cloud company and Nvidia customer that has been separately accused of facilitating restricted chip exports to China.

Huang will remain as CEO during the investigation. Three House Republicans have already demanded answers on his interactions and Nvidia's foreign-espionage safeguards. Nvidia shares fell ~5% in premarket Monday morning. China's Foreign Ministry called the espionage allegation "unsubstantiated."

The probe comes nine days before Nvidia's November 17 earnings — timing that adds to analyst uncertainty about whether Huang could be removed before the results are reported.

Sources: [CNBC (Nov 9)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-jensen-huang-chinese-spy-investigation.html), [CNBC (Nov 8)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-jensen-huang-investigation-china-spy.html), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/11/08/nvidia-opens-investigation-into-ceo-over-relationship-with-suspected-chinese-spy/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidias-jensen-huang-admits-to-affair-after-allegations-of-romance-with-chinese-spy-report-205122263.html)

## Policy

### [UPDATE] Trump Super Intelligence Task Force: AI prosecution coordination + state law challenge added to mandate

**Background:** The Super Intelligence Task Force was announced October 4, led by DNI Jay Clayton, with a 120-day mandate to assess AI risks and opportunities. No statutory authority; approach centered on voluntary accords and incident reporting.

**New:** Per today's digest reporting, the task force now has two active operational jobs: (1) coordinating AI-related federal prosecutions, and (2) formally challenging certain state AI laws it deems inconsistent with federal policy. Separately, an AP story reports that U.S. AI safety center staff are now informally reporting to the White House, suggesting the administration is consolidating AI oversight into the executive rather than keeping it at the NIST/AISI layer.

Sources: [The Neuron (Nov 9)](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-november-9-2026/), [Rio Rundown](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-nov-09-2026-ai-agents-and)
