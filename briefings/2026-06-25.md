## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic Accuses Alibaba of Largest-Ever Distillation Attack on Claude

Anthropic sent letters to Senate Banking Committee chairs Tim Scott and Elizabeth Warren, and to the White House, accusing Alibaba of orchestrating a "brazenly" and "illicitly" coordinated capability-extraction campaign against Claude. According to the letter (dated June 10, published by [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html) and [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-illicitly-accessing-its-ai-models) on June 24), Alibaba Qwen used approximately 25,000 fraudulent accounts to generate 28.8 million Claude exchanges between April 22 and June 5, 2026, specifically targeting Claude's software engineering and agentic reasoning capabilities. Anthropic calls it the largest known distillation attack it has faced, and notes it's part of a broader pattern — three prior industrial-scale campaigns from DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax were identified in February 2026. Alibaba has not responded publicly.

**Why it matters:** Distillation attacks are the exact threat model that motivates Anthropic's Mythos-access restrictions. The scale here (28.8M exchanges, 25K accounts) makes it a qualitatively different threat from opportunistic scraping. The Senate targeting likely positions this for legislative action on model-theft specifically, distinct from the broader Fable 5 / export control fight.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-illicitly-accessing-its-ai-models), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/60088712/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-ai-model-theft-says-28-8-million-claude-conversations-were-harvested).

### [NEW] Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves DeepMind for Anthropic

John Jumper — Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024, co-creator of AlphaFold — announced June 19-20 that he's leaving Google DeepMind after nearly nine years to join Anthropic, after a recharge period. Jumper led the AlphaFold team and is one of the most credentialed ML researchers in structural biology. His departure follows DeepMind VP of Engineering Noam Shazeer's move to OpenAI days prior, signaling accelerating talent churn at DeepMind. Jumper has not disclosed his role at Anthropic, stating on X: "I am incredibly grateful for my time at GDM. @demishassabis took a real chance letting me lead the AlphaFold team just six months after finishing..."

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/nobel-winner-john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic).

## Model Releases

### [NEW] OpenAI GPT-5.5-Cyber: Restricted Defensive Security Model

OpenAI released GPT-5.5-Cyber on June 22 under the Daybreak "Trusted Access for Cyber" program — access gated to vetted defenders only, not a public API. Partners include Akamai, Cisco, Cloudflare, and CrowdStrike, plus government agencies in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Poland, South Korea, and EU institutions. Benchmarks: 85.6% on CyberGym (vs. 81.8% for GPT-5.5), 39.5% on ExploitGym, 69.8% on SEC-bench Pro. Capabilities include full automation of vulnerability detection and patching, large-codebase reachability analysis, and evidence preparation for human review. No public pricing announced. [Sources: [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-with-trusted-access-for-cyber/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/22/openai-rolls-out-more-capable-version-of-cyber-model), [Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/06/openai-expands-daybreak-with-gpt-55.html)]

**Why it matters:** Gated defensive security models are becoming a separate product tier (parallel to Anthropic's Mythos Preview / Glasswing structure). If your work touches security tooling, this is the path — not the public API.
