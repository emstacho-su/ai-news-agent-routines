## Anthropic

### [NEW] Model Hardware Standard (MHS): research preview

Anthropic announced the research preview of the Model Hardware Standard on August 27, a shared specification that lets AI agents safely operate physical equipment — microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, quantum-computer calibration rigs — in parallel without bespoke integration. MHS works with any device that has a programmable interface and is model-agnostic: any agent harness accessing it via standard protocols including MCP can drive it. Anthropic says integration time drops from weeks to hours. Development began in collaboration with HHMI Janelia Research Campus; Anthropic plans to open-source the spec. Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-pushes-into-physical-world-with-new-standard-to-help-ai-agents-operate-machines.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-makes-first-move-into-physical-ai-with-universal-standard-for-scientists-manufacturing/).

**Why it matters:** If MHS gets adoption, it positions MCP-native agent stacks (Claude Code included) as the control layer for wet labs and manufacturing lines — a much larger surface than pure software tasks.

### [NEW] Claude Memory unified across Chat + Cowork — editable, sensitive-subject opt-in

Announced August 25, Claude Memory now persists across both Chat and Cowork sessions: what Claude learns in one context carries into the other automatically. The redesigned memory UI exposes stored information topic-by-topic; users can open, edit, delete, pause, or reset individual topics. Sensitive subjects — health, race/ethnicity, gender identity, religion, political beliefs — are excluded from memory by default but can be opted in per-topic. Sources: [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/claudes-memory-works-everywhere-and-you-decide-whats-in-it), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/claude-cowork-finally-remembers-what-you-told-the-app-in-chat/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2243753/claude-memory-now-works-across-both-chats-and-cowork-sessions/).

### [NEW] Claude for Scientists: 10,000 seats + AI for Science credits expand

On August 27, Anthropic opened 10,000 verified researcher seats on Claude Team: standard access free, premium (5× usage limits) at $15/month fixed for one year. Eligibility requires a principal investigator at an academic or nonprofit institution, who can then add lab members. Anthropic also expanded its AI for Science credit program from biological sciences to every scientific field. The announcement confirmed that Mythos — Anthropic's restricted-model tier — has enrolled its first participants in life sciences research, separate from the general seat program. Sources: [Anthropic](https://claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-opens-10000-free-and-discounted-claude-seats-for-scientists/).

**Why it matters:** Direct researcher access to Mythos-class capabilities in a gated academic channel is a signal about where the "trusted partner" rollout goes next.

## Model Releases

### [UPDATE] GPT-5.6 Sol: 20% API price cut through November

**Background:** OpenAI had already cut GPT-5.6 Luna 80% and Terra 20% in mid-August.  
**New:** Sol now joins the reduction — input drops from $5 to $4, output from $30 to $20 per million tokens. The promotional rate runs through at least November 21 and applies to the pay-as-you-go API, Codex credits, and eligible ChatGPT Work plans; consumer subscriptions (Pro, Plus, Business) are unchanged. This is the second price cut to the GPT-5.6 family in under a month. Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6/), [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/openai-gpt-56-sol-price-cut-20-percent-frontier-model-august-2026/), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421074).

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] DeepMind "Visual General Intelligence" white paper

Google DeepMind published "Visual General Intelligence: A White Paper" on August 26, proposing VGI — defined as the capacity to acquire usable world knowledge directly from visual experience and apply it to prediction, imagination, and action — as a concrete pathway toward AGI. The paper is listed on DeepMind's official publications page and is accessible via arXiv aggregators. Full methodology details are in the paper itself; the high-level claim is that vision-grounded world models are a more tractable route to generalizable intelligence than text-only or multimodal-fusion approaches. Sources: [DeepMind Publications](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/), [deeplearn.org](https://deeplearn.org/arxiv/812875/visual-general-intelligence:-a-white-paper).
