## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Model Hardware Standard gains hardware partners, waitlist open

**Background:** Anthropic published a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS) on Aug 27 — a spec letting AI agents safely operate physical lab and manufacturing equipment (microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms) via standard read/write drivers instead of bespoke integrations, cutting integration time from weeks to hours.

**New:** MHS is now in waitlist access with named hardware partners: Universal Robots (robot arms), Tecan (lab liquid handling), and AWS (cloud control plane). The spec is model-agnostic — not locked to Claude — and Anthropic has confirmed it will be open-sourced. Elizabeth Kelly, Anthropic's head of beneficial deployments, framed it as moving into physical territory that rivals have been buying into.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-pushes-into-physical-world-with-new-standard-to-help-ai-agents-operate-machines.html), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-makes-first-move-into-physical-ai-with-universal-standard-for-scientists-manufacturing/), [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/anthropic-previews-new-standard-to-streamline-ai-to-machine-connections/)

**Why it matters:** If MHS adoption tracks the way MCP did, it's worth watching now. The lab-instrument integration problem is real and ugly; if this becomes the USB-C for scientific hardware, Claude gets first-mover advantage in agentic lab automation.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] OpenAI Jalapeño chip posts first public benchmark results at Hot Chips 2026

**Background:** Jalapeño is OpenAI's custom inference ASIC, co-designed with Broadcom and unveiled in June 2026. Initial specs claimed ~50% cost reduction per inference vs current GPUs; production deployment targeted end of 2026, with full ramp in 2027.

**New:** At Hot Chips 2026 (Aug 25-26), OpenAI published first official benchmark results via SemiAnalysis's InferenceX benchmark. Jalapeño recorded higher tokens-per-user and more throughput-per-kilowatt than current state-of-the-art GPU systems — and did both simultaneously, where existing hardware typically trades one for the other. The chip targets the prefill and communication bottlenecks. End-2026 deployment is "very small volumes"; scale-up starts in 2027 and ramps "full tilt in first half '28."

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/openais-jalapeno-chip-is-built-for-fast-inference-at-scale-benchmarks-shows/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/openai-jalapeno-ai-chip-nvidia.html), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/08/25/openais-upcoming-jalapeno-chip-looks-like-itll-be-an-inference-beast/5292052)

## Model Releases

### [UPDATE] ChatGPT free tier: GPT-5.6 Luna only, unlimited chats, Think button

**Background:** GPT-5.6 Luna had its API price cut to $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens (was $1/$6) in late July; Sol API also cut to $4/$20 in August.

**New:** On the product side, OpenAI has consolidated ChatGPT's free tier to a single model — GPT-5.6 Luna — and removed the session message cap (unlimited text chats). A Think button gives free users limited access to reasoning mode. Separately, OpenAI published an update improving GPT-5.6 Sol (coherence, instruction-following) and explicitly expanding Luna access as a tier stepping stone. GPT-5.4 mini remains available via the Thinking menu for free/Go users as a rate-limit fallback.

Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/improving-gpt-5-6-sol-in-chatgpt/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2234173/chatgpt-free-features-2026/), [Medium](https://medium.com/ai-tomorrow/openai-just-gave-free-users-access-to-their-most-powerful-thinking-model-e42d50170edc)

## Wildcard

### [NEW] AWS shutting down Mechanical Turk on Sept 30 after 21 years

Amazon announced it will close Mechanical Turk on September 30, 2026 — the 2005-era crowdsourced task marketplace Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence." At its peak the platform served 500,000+ workers on Human Intelligence Tasks ranging from data labeling to transcription. Closing alongside it: SageMaker Ground Truth and Amazon Augmented AI, effectively Amazon's full exit from human-data infrastructure. Amazon cited an "internal assessment" but the proximate cause is plain: modern data labeling startups (Scale AI, Mercor, Prolific) and AI-generated synthetic data have displaced the model. 500,000 workers had 5 weeks' notice.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/amazon-service-that-jeff-bezos-called-artificial-ai-is-shutting-down.html), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/amazon-mechanical-turk-closing-september-2026), [Quartz](https://qz.com/amazon-mechanical-turk-shutting-down-082626), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/325645/20260826/amazon-mechanical-turk-will-close-september-30-shutting-down-sagemaker-ground-truth-too.htm)

**Why it matters:** Mechanical Turk trained a non-trivial fraction of the LLMs you use daily. Its closure is a clean marker that synthetic data + AI-in-the-loop has crossed the threshold where human crowdsourcing at this scale isn't economically viable. Worth citing if you're explaining the AI labor stack to anyone.
