## Anthropic

### [NEW] Claude Tag: persistent AI teammate lives inside Slack

Anthropic launched Claude Tag in beta on June 23 for Team and Enterprise plans. Unlike the previous Claude-in-Slack app, Tag joins channels as a named team member with persistent context from channel history and connected tools. Anyone in the channel can @-mention to delegate tasks. "Ambient" mode lets Claude proactively surface relevant information across channels and follow up on stalled threads without explicit prompting. Tool connections include Jira, GitHub, Google Drive, and others via MCP connectors. Existing Claude for Slack users have a 30-day opt-in migration window.

Internally, Anthropic reports 65% of its product team's code is now written by their own Tag deployment — the clearest public signal yet about Anthropic's internal AI-first engineering culture. Works with Opus 4.8 (Fable 5 suspended). [Anthropic announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/23/anthropic-claude-tag-virtual-employee-tool-slack/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-claude-tag-slack-always-on-ai-teammate)

**Why it matters:** The ambient mode + multi-channel context model is a real design signal for how Anthropic thinks human-AI teaming works in practice. If you're building enterprise workflows on Claude, this is the reference implementation to watch.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Oracle SEC filing names AI as the explicit cause of 21,000 layoffs

Oracle's fiscal 2026 10-K (filed June 23 with the SEC) is the first regulatory filing from a major technology company to state directly that AI deployment caused workforce reductions. Headcount fell from ~162,000 to ~141,000 over the fiscal year — 13% of total staff — while Oracle simultaneously expanded AI data center commitments with OpenAI and Meta. The filing states: "the adoption and deployment of AI technologies across our operations have resulted, and may continue to result, in reductions to our workforce." Oracle spent $1.84B on restructuring in fiscal 2026, up from $374M the year prior. Most tech companies attribute cuts to "efficiency" or "business restructuring"; having lawyers sign off on "AI" as the named driver in an SEC filing is a materially different liability posture. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/oracle-ai-job-cuts-layoffs-21000.html), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/oracle-21000-layoffs-ai-data-centres), [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/112864-oracle-cuts-21000-jobs-admits-ai-reducing-workforce.html)

### [UPDATE] Altman: RSI within 6 months could favor delaying OpenAI's IPO

In internal communications surfaced June 24, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told staff he believes recursive self-improvement could arrive within six months, and that hitting this milestone before the IPO window "might favor staying private." Direct quote: "The faster the potential RSI takeoff looks like it could be, the more it could be advantageous to delay an IPO — because the technology and the world may change in surprising ways, and there might be good reasons to be a private company during that time." Internal milestones: automated intern-level function by September 2026, full "automated AI researcher" by March 2028. The May 2026 confidential S-1 still targets a September listing; no delay has been announced.
