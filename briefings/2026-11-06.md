## Anthropic

### [NEW] FlashProve: ZK Proofs for AI Model Identity Verification

Anthropic released FlashProve on November 2, 2026 as an open-source research prototype (Apache 2.0) that uses zkSNARKs to prove a hosted AI response came from committed model weights — without exposing the weights. Collaborators include EPFL, MIT, StarkWare, and Succinct; soundness guarantees are formally verified in Lean 4. On 4-bit Llama 4 Maverick, proof generation runs ~110 tokens/sec on a single H100 GPU but costs 70-95x the GPU time of running inference alone; verification runs in ~17 seconds on CPU with 24 threads. Constraints: 1,024-token contexts, greedy decoding only, MoE architectures unsupported.

**Why it matters:** FlashProve addresses a real problem Stack will hit as a Claude Code developer — there's currently no way for an operator or auditor to verify that a provider is running the model they claim. The proof establishes the commitment, though mapping that commitment to a product name still requires out-of-band authentication. Not a deployed API and "not maintained, not accepting contributions" per the repo.

Sources: [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-flashprove-zksnark-ai-inference/), [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-flashprove-cryptographically-proves-which-ai-model-answered-you), [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/read/news/anthropic-unveils-flashprove-for-proving-which-model-answered-you).

## Wildcard

### [UPDATE] Data Center Ballot Measures: Near-Total Sweep Against AI Infrastructure

**Background:** 41 local ballot measures across 10 states voted November 3 on restricting or banning data center construction.

**New (official results, unofficial tallies):** Ohio's 21 measures went almost entirely against data centers. Upper Sandusky added a ban to its charter: 77% yes. Granville, Pataskala, and Sunbury banned facilities over 25 MW, each at ≥74%. Ashville voters overturned a previously approved EdgeConneX project at 82% yes. Wilmington: ~80% against rezoning across four referendums. Monroeville, the only community with a *pro*-development advisory measure, rejected it 88% no. Commissioner recalls passed in Piqua (~70%+), Urbana's mayor also recalled over a data center dispute. Nationwide, only Harwood, ND reportedly bucked the trend. Fortune tallies at least 14 Ohio municipalities with new bans or restrictions; Ballotpedia's broader count across all states is 41 total.

Sources: [Reason](https://reason.com/2026/11/04/ohio-leads-a-wave-of-local-data-center-bans/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/04/ohio-data-center-ballot-measures-midterm-elections/), [Axios Columbus](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2026/11/05/ohio-data-center-election).

## Practical Tips

**Testing FlashProve locally:** The repo is at version 0.16.0 and runs against 4-bit quantized models. If you want to experiment: `git clone` the Apache 2.0 repo, run against a local Llama 4 Maverick checkpoint. Budget ~70-95x your normal inference GPU time for proof generation. Useful context: the proof size is ~19 MB per response; verification is CPU-only and takes ~17s with thread parallelism.
