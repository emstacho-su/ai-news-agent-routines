## Research & Technical

### [NEW] Stanford AI100: scaling is running out of road

Stanford published its third five-year AI100 report, *AI Goes Mainstream: The Scaling Era and the Rise of Generative AI*, on November 10. The core finding: scaling (more compute and data) drove most of generative AI's capability gains, but is now hitting concrete constraints — scarce high-quality training data and prohibitive frontier-training costs. The panel, chaired by Oxford's Michael Wooldridge, covers 12 standing questions plus two deep dives on AI geopolitics and scaling.

Separately, the annual AI Index 2026 (also Stanford HAI) finds generative AI hit 53% global adoption in three years — faster diffusion than the PC or the internet.

**Why it matters:** This is the most authoritative independent five-year assessment of the generative AI arc. The scaling-limits finding maps to what practitioners are observing: returns are diminishing, and progress is increasingly a function of efficiency improvements and new approaches (reasoning models, agentic composition, weight-level editing). The Stanford panel is not saying scaling is dead — they're saying it needs to be paired with efficiency doublings. Worth watching the follow-on academic coverage.

Sources: [thenextweb](https://thenextweb.com/news/stanford-ai100-report-2026-ai-goes-mainstream), [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/stanfords-ai100-panel-publishes-third-five-year-report-on-ai-15954eba), [cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/stanford-ai100-report-ai-scaling-limits/).

### [NEW] MIT CSAIL: LLMs can directly edit model weights without training

Paper: *Sparks of Weight-Space Programming* (Takehi, Teoh, Zhang, Khattab; MIT CSAIL, arXiv preprint, November 2, 2026). The setup: GPT-6 Astra is given a natural-language goal and the raw weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B; it edits the weights directly — no optimizer, no training data, no gradient descent. It edited 0.016% of parameters and raised MATH-500 accuracy from 7.9% to 48.1% (+40pp). GSM8K went from 13.8% to 67.1%. Side effect: IFEval (instruction following) improved ~3× without being targeted. In a separate experiment, Astra wrote weights for three game-playing neural nets from rules and source code alone; all three beat PPO baselines trained for 100M steps.

Limitations the authors flag: editing can unlock latent abilities but can't install capabilities the model fundamentally lacks. Backdoor removal and capability unlearning remain on the future-work list. Code and logs are reportedly on GitHub.

**Why it matters:** If this generalizes beyond small models, it opens a new tool for targeted model steering — fixing a specific failure mode without retraining. The preprint numbers are on 0.8B weights, not production scale. Still: the MATH-500 jump from a 0.016% edit is a signal worth tracking.

Sources: [blog.gopenai.com](https://blog.gopenai.com/weight-space-programming-explained-llms-that-edit-weights-35ef19e10290), [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/gpt-6-astra-edited-0-016-percent-qwen-weights-math-sixfold), [xenospectrum](https://xenospectrum.com/en/sparks-of-weight-space-programming-mit-csail/).
