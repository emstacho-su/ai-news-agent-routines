## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Fable 5 + Mythos 5 ban: still down, weeks-long timeline

**Background:** US Commerce Secretary Lutnick's June 12 export directive forced Anthropic to pull both Fable 5 and Mythos 5 globally. Anthropic met with White House/Commerce officials June 15 without resolution.

**New:** As of June 17, models remain offline. Anthropic has told affected enterprise partners restoration could take **weeks rather than days**, and no date has been given. Anthropic's public posture remains that the cited jailbreak "provides no Mythos-specific uplift" and that the standard applied would freeze all new frontier-model deployments industry-wide. The Fable 5 free-access window (originally June 9–22) is effectively consumed by the outage.

Sources: [explainx.ai restoration timeline](https://explainx.ai/blog/when-will-fable-5-be-available-again-2026), [Anthropic statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access), [FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/anthropic-says-trump-ban-puts-federal-contractor-partnerships-jeopardy/).

**Why it matters:** If you're building on Fable 5 or Mythos 5 via API, the outage is ongoing. All traffic continues to route to Opus 4.8 automatically. Factor this into any production timeline.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [NEW] Databricks Data + AI Summit: OpenSharing protocol + Unity AI Gateway

Databricks' annual summit (June 15–18, Moscone Center, San Francisco, 30K+ in person) landed two announcements relevant to agent infrastructure. First, Databricks and the Linux Foundation jointly launched **OpenSharing**, an open vendor-neutral protocol for sharing AI assets — agent skills, ML models, and unstructured data — across organizational and platform boundaries. Second, **Unity AI Gateway** brings models, agents, tools, and MCP servers under a single runtime governance layer with unified policy enforcement. Agent Bricks, the platform's agent-building layer, now reports 100K+ agents built and over 1 quadrillion tokens processed per year. Satya Nadella joined in a pre-recorded keynote alongside Greg Brockman.

Sources: [Databricks OpenSharing + Agent Bricks blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/agent-bricks-dais-2026), [Azure Databricks announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azure-databricks/azure-databricks-at-databricks-data--ai-summit-2026-updates-and-new-announcement/4528388), [TechTimes Day 2 recap](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318450/20260616/databricks-summit-2026-day-2-agentic-ai-catalog-federation-move-lab-enterprise.htm).

**Why it matters:** OpenSharing is an attempt to do for agent skills what OCI did for containers — portable, governed, cross-vendor. If it gains traction, MCP servers you build today could be discoverable through a standard registry, not locked to one vendor's marketplace.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] NVIDIA Ising: open-source AI for quantum error correction (April 14 — missed)

NVIDIA released the Ising family of open-source AI models on April 14, not previously covered here. There are two components: **Ising Calibration**, a vision-language model that automates quantum processor calibration (reducing calibration cycles from days to hours), and **Ising Decoding**, models for quantum error correction that run 2.5× faster and achieve 3× higher accuracy than the pyMatching open-source baseline. Released on GitHub, Hugging Face, and build.nvidia.com; integrates with CUDA-Q and NVQLink. Early adopters include IonQ, IQM, Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory, and Harvard. The models are purpose-built for hybrid quantum-classical workflows, not general-purpose LLMs.

Sources: [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-ising-the-worlds-first-open-ai-models-to-accelerate-the-path-to-useful-quantum-computers), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-releases-ising-open-ai-models), [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/04/14/nvidia-launches-ising-the-worlds-first-open-ai-models-to-accelerate-the-path-to-useful-quantum-computers/).

## Research & Technical

### [NEW] OpenAI Deployment Simulation: replaying real conversations before release

OpenAI published Deployment Simulation on June 16 ([blog post](https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation/)). The technique strips the assistant response from recent production conversations and regenerates them using a candidate model before release — giving a more ecologically valid preview of model behavior than adversarial test sets. The study analyzed ~1.3M de-identified conversations spanning GPT-5 Thinking through GPT-5.4 (August 2025–March 2026) across 20 undesired-behavior categories. Aggregate result: a median multiplicative estimation error of 1.5× (i.e., if true rate is 10-per-100K, the technique estimates 6.7–15). It doesn't replace red-teaming but adds a feedback loop from real deployment patterns.

Sources: [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/16/openai-deployment-simulation/), [AI Daily Post](https://aidailypost.com/news/openais-deployment-simulation-beats-baseline-adds-risk-checks-agentic).

**Why it matters:** This is reproducible — you could run the same technique against your own logged assistant conversations to estimate regression rates before swapping models.
