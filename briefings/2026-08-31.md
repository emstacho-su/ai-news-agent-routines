## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Claude Sonnet 5 — $2/$10 pricing confirmed permanent

**Background:** When Sonnet 5 launched in April 2026 Anthropic set $2/M input, $10/M output as introductory pricing through August 31, with $3/$15 as the stated September 1 standard rate.

**New:** The standard-rate increase has been canceled. Anthropic announced on August 10 that introductory pricing is now the permanent pricing. As of today, no step-up will occur. Separately, Anthropic deleted a Claude Code blog post and republished it with corrected framing around the "17% cost cut" characterization — the original post understated the new-tokenizer overhead that partly offsets the price reduction.

**Why it matters:** For anything hitting the API at volume — Routines, agent pipelines, batch jobs — no need to re-cost after September. The tokenizer overhead note is still live: Sonnet 5's tokenizer encodes the same text as 0–35% more tokens than Sonnet 4.6, so effective cost at constant token-output budgets is still higher unless you've already profiled your workloads.

### [NEW] Model Hardware Standard (MHS) — AI agents for physical devices

Anthropic opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS) on August 27. MHS is a shared protocol spec that lets AI agents operate physical lab instruments and manufacturing equipment — microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, laser calibration rigs — the way USB-C standardized data transfer across device types. The framing: integrating AI with a single instrument currently takes days to weeks of bespoke driver/adapter work; MHS cuts that to hours.

The spec is device-agnostic: anything with a programmable interface can implement it. Agents operating over MHS can coordinate multiple instruments in parallel. Research preview partners include Genentech, Carnegie Mellon, AWS, Doosan Robotics, Tecan, Universal Robots, Hugging Face, and Raspberry Pi. Anthropic plans to open-source the spec after the preview period.

**Why it matters:** This is the most concrete move by a frontier lab to turn AI agents into first-class participants in wet-lab and manufacturing workflows, not just code editors. If MHS gets adoption it becomes the MCP-equivalent for physical-world integration — same "shared discoverable interface" model that MCP used for software tools.

Sources: [Anthropic MHS announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview), [CNBC — physical world push](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-pushes-into-physical-world-with-new-standard-to-help-ai-agents-operate-machines.html), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/29/anthropic-opens-a-research-preview-of-the-model-hardware-standard-mhs-a-shared-specification-for-ai-agents-to-safely-operate-physical-devices/)

### [NEW] Sony and Warner sue Anthropic in multi-billion-dollar copyright suit

Filed August 29 in the US District Court for the Northern District of California: Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Music allege Anthropic conducted "one of the largest and most blatant ongoing thefts of intellectual property in history" — torrenting, scraping, and bulk-downloading copyrighted musical compositions to train Claude. Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann are named individually as defendants. The suit seeks statutory damages of up to $150,000 per willfully infringed work plus up to $25,000 per copyright management information removal.

Context: This is the third and fourth music-publisher suit in the chain. BMG sued in March 2026 (493 compositions), Round Hill Music filed August 17. Sony/Warner's suit is the highest-profile addition to date. Anthropic's response: "We disagree with the publishers' claims and we intend to defend ourselves robustly in court."

### [NEW] Claude in Chrome — autonomous browser actions GA on paid plans

Generally available as of August 26: Claude in Chrome (the official browser extension) is now live for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers. The GA build ships autonomous action mode — Claude can take a sequence of browser actions (read text, click, type, navigate, fill forms) without approval gating every step. A safety classifier validates each proposed action before execution. Cowork sessions also now run as a Chrome side panel alongside any page. The extension uses your existing logins (no separate credential handoff).

**Why it matters:** This moves Claude from "reads the page you share" to "operates the browser on your behalf" — closer to a real computer-use surface than the prior request-approve loop. The safety classifier architecture is the thing to watch: Anthropic is betting on inline action-level classification rather than blanket whitelisting.

### [NEW] OpenAI terminates Cursor model access after SpaceX acquisition — Nov 12 cutoff

OpenAI announced August 29 it is terminating its commercial model-access agreement with Cursor, effective November 12, 2026. The trigger: Anysphere (Cursor's parent) was acquired by Elon Musk's SpaceX. OpenAI invoked a change-of-control clause in the contract, citing inability to trust that SpaceX will operate within OpenAI's terms of service. OpenAI's stated basis: Twitter violated terms after Musk's acquisition, and xAI used OpenAI training data under oath-acknowledged TOS violations. OpenAI says it will not supply future models to Cursor as the wind-down proceeds, and framed the November 12 date as the maximum notice window the contract allows to give developers time to migrate.

**Why it matters:** Cursor is one of the highest-volume OpenAI API consumers in the developer tools space; the cutoff affects any developer workflow built on GPT models via Cursor specifically. Cursor's team will presumably pivot to Anthropic (already available in Cursor) and potentially xAI/Grok as alternatives. The "change-of-control = contract termination" precedent is notable for any AI API integrator tracking acquisition risk.

Sources: [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html), [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-terminates-contract-with-cursor-following-acquisition-by-elon-musks-spacex/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2246969/openai-pull-its-models-from-cursor-due-to-spacexai-acquisition/)

## Practical Tips

- **Budget for Sonnet 5 now:** The $2/$10 pricing is permanent — if you've been holding off on committing Sonnet 5 as the default for agent pipelines pending confirmation of the September rate, that uncertainty is resolved.
- **Cursor migration window:** If any of your tooling depends on Cursor + GPT-4o/GPT-5.x via OpenAI, November 12 is the hard cutoff. Claude-in-Cursor is already live and functional; now is the time to test it as the fallback before you need it.
