### [UPDATE] Fable 5 suspension: June 29 status and Annex A fine print

**Background:** Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were suspended June 12 by US Commerce Department export-control directive; June 26 Lutnick letter partially lifted the block for Mythos 5 only. Axios reported June 27 that Fable 5 is "on track to return soon," pending Pentagon/NSA sign-off.

**New (June 29):** Fable 5 remains offline for all general users, API developers, Claude Code, and international subscribers. [isfable5back.com](https://isfable5back.com/) — No. Prediction markets closed the week at 68–71% odds for restore before July 1, with roughly 48 hours left on the clock and no product change.

Annex A fine print from the Lutnick June 26 letter (now fully public): Mythos 5 is cleared for three categories without individual export license: (1) the Annex A list of US critical-infrastructure entities **and** their foreign-national employees; (2) Anthropic's own foreign-national staff; (3) US government civilian agencies and national labs. General API access, Claude Code integrations, and non-Annex-A international customers remain blocked. Anthropic's official post confirms they are "continuing to work with the government to expand access to Mythos 5 and make Fable 5 available for general use again."

Sources: [isfable5back.com](https://isfable5back.com/), [explainx.ai — Annex A details](https://explainx.ai/blog/is-fable-5-back-2026), [Anthropic X post](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2070665903440871779), [Axios June 27 scoop](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-fable-5-return-soon).

**Why it matters:** If you're scoping Routines work that needs Fable 5: until there's an official Anthropic announcement, plan against Opus 4.8 as the ceiling. The Annex A carve-out for foreign-national employees of qualifying US orgs is notable — if Stack's university or a co-op employer is on the list, that's a restore path separate from the July 8 Persona ID verification route.

### [UPDATE] Claude Code: OpenTelemetry telemetry + Trusted Devices for Remote Control

**Background:** Late June shipped `claude agents` CLI subcommand for inspecting active sessions, plus mouse-control and background-agent reliability fixes (covered June 28).

**New:** Two additional features confirmed in the June 2026 release documentation:

**OpenTelemetry telemetry output** — Claude Code now exports structured telemetry via standard OTel signals. Enable with `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_TELEMETRY=1` plus your exporter env vars (`OTEL_METRICS_EXPORTER`, `OTEL_LOGS_EXPORTER`, `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`, etc.). Signals tracked out of the box: token usage and costs per session, session/request activity, performance (latency, streaming), quota and rate-limit hits, tool invocation decisions, and errors/retries. The new `claude_code.assistant_response` log event includes model response text, redacted unless `OTEL_LOG_ASSISTANT_RESPONSES=1`.

**Trusted Devices for Remote Control Admins** — Team and Enterprise admins can now require members to complete device verification before their Claude Code session can be viewed or steered remotely. Reduces lateral-movement risk if a Remote Control admin token is compromised.

Also confirmed: live file path autocomplete in bash mode.

Sources: [Claude Code changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/whats-new), [SigNoz OTel docs](https://signoz.io/docs/claude-code-monitoring/), [Releasebot June 2026](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code).

**Why it matters:** OTel telemetry is the right hook for watching token costs across Routines runs — you can pipe it straight to Grafana or Elastic without any custom instrumentation. Trusted Devices matters if you're sharing Remote Control access in a team environment.

## Wildcard

### [NEW] Colorado's original AI Act quietly replaced — SB 26-189 takes effect January 2027

Colorado's SB 24-205 — the first comprehensive state AI law in the US, scheduled to take effect June 30, 2026 — is dead. A federal district court stayed it April 27 after the DOJ joined a constitutional challenge. Governor Polis then signed SB 26-189 on May 14, 2026, repealing and replacing the original law.

SB 26-189 strips the original's mandatory risk-management programs, annual impact assessments, and broad algorithmic discrimination duties. What remains: four narrower operational duties for automated decision-making technology (ADMT) used in "consequential decisions":

1. Notify users when interacting with AI
2. Disclose to consumers within 30 days of an adverse ADMT outcome
3. Correct inaccurate personal data on request
4. Provide meaningful human review and reconsideration

Effective January 1, 2027. Scope is narrowed to consequential-decision ADMT — not general AI products.

Sources: [Colorado General Assembly SB26-189](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb26-189), [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/colorado-hits-reset-on-ai-regulation-sb-26-189-repeals-and-reenacts-the-colorado-ai-act), [Consumer Finance Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/05/12/colorado-rewrites-its-landmark-ai-law-unpacking-sb-26-189-and-what-it-means-for-businesses/).
