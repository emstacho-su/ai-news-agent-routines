## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic, Amazon, Microsoft, Google co-develop shared jailbreak severity scoring
Anthropic announced a cross-company initiative with Amazon, Microsoft, and Google to create a standardized jailbreak risk scoring framework for frontier models. The shared rubric assigns severity scores to jailbreak disclosures across vendor model families, replacing the current situation where each organization (and each downstream buyer) independently assesses the same finding. The framework is expected to be positioned as a voluntary industry standard in an August 1 announcement.

**Why it matters:** If you're doing red-teaming or evaluating AI tooling for a security-sensitive project, this is the first time you'll have a cross-vendor, apples-to-apples severity signal rather than each lab's self-assessed disclosure — directly useful when writing procurement security requirements.

### [UPDATE] GPT-5.6 full GA + ChatGPT Work launch
**Background:** GPT-5.6 had been in a government-reviewed limited preview since June 26 following Trump administration export restrictions.

**New:** Full public GA hit July 9 across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API. The model ships as a three-tier family — Sol (flagship, "best coding model yet," 54% more token-efficient on agentic coding jobs), Terra (intermediate; half the cost of GPT-5.5 equivalents), and Luna (speed-optimized, lowest cost). Alongside it, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work: merges ChatGPT, Codex, and the Atlas browser into a single desktop product, powered by GPT-5.6, that can stay on multi-step tasks for hours across a 15-integration plugin directory. Available on Mac/Windows desktop for all users; Plus/Pro get web access too.

**Why it matters:** Sol's 54% agentic coding efficiency gain is a concrete production metric, not a benchmark — relevant if you're evaluating whether to route coding workloads through API vs Copilot.

Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/ai-openai-gpt-release), [InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4195478/openai-launches-chatgpt-work-as-it-broadens-gpt-5-6-rollout.html), [TechBriefly](https://techbriefly.com/2026/07/10/chatgpt-work-gpt-5-6-productivity-app/)

## Wildcard

### [NEW] SambaNova closes $1B Series F at $11B valuation; JPMorganChase deploys on-prem
SambaNova completed the first close of a $1B Series F on July 8, led by General Atlantic at an $11B post-money valuation. Investors include BlackRock, Intel Capital, Qatar Investment Authority, T. Rowe Price, Battery Ventures, and Vista Equity. JPMorganChase was named as an anchor customer, deploying SN40 and SN50 systems for secure on-premises AI inference at the bank. CEO Rodrigo Liang confirmed a second close is expected within weeks.

**Why it matters:** On-prem inference for regulated industries is maturing into its own market segment. JPMorgan's public commitment signals that even Tier-1 banks are treating AI inference as infrastructure rather than a cloud API subscription — the kind of signal that moves compliance roadmaps at other FIs.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-draws-1b-at-11b-valuation-in-series-f-first-close/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/ai-chip-startup-sambanova-raises-funds-at-11-billion-valuation/), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/sambanova-raises-1bn-in-series-f-round-ai-compute-company-valued-at-11bn/)

### [NEW] Google Africa Applied AI Lab opens in Accra
Google launched the Africa Applied AI Lab in Accra, Ghana, providing African researchers and entrepreneurs early access to Google AI technologies and direct technical mentorship from Google engineers. The program targets development of AI solutions specific to African infrastructure and context constraints — distinct from existing Google.org grant programs.
