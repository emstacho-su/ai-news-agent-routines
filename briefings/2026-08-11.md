## Anthropic

### [NEW] Anthropic, Macquarie, and GIC Launch Theseus Infrastructure Data Center JV

Anthropic announced a strategic joint venture with Macquarie Asset Management and GIC (Singapore's sovereign wealth fund) called Theseus Infrastructure, purpose-built to fund and develop AI data centers with an initial focus on the US. Macquarie and GIC own the platform and fund the majority of equity per project; Anthropic is the anchor tenant and has pledged to cover 100% of grid-upgrade costs and any consumer electricity price increases tied to its data center demand. Each facility will be built to Anthropic's capacity specifications. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/anthropic-macquarie-and-gic-form-venture-for-ai-data-centers), [Macquarie Group](https://www.macquarie.com/au/en/about/news/2026/anthropic-mam-gic-data-centre-infrastructure-partnership.html), [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/anthropic-macquarie-and-gic-launch-theseus-infrastructure-for-ai-data-centers/)

**Why it matters:** Anthropic continues building out independent compute infrastructure outside its AWS and Google Cloud agreements — Theseus is the third leg, and the first where Anthropic controls the siting decisions. Combined with the Volta ($10B cloud startup deal, Aug 4), AMD MI450 deal, and in-house chip team, this week's moves mark a meaningful shift toward infrastructure self-determination.

## Model Releases

### [NEW] Meta Muse Glimmer: Open-Weight 30B On-Device Agentic Model

Meta released Muse Glimmer on August 10, a 30B-parameter model distilled from Muse Spark 1.2 and released under Apache 2.0. Unlike Muse Spark (cloud API only, closed-weight), Glimmer is designed to run on a single consumer GPU — a Mac or PC — for always-on local agent workflows. It supports tool use, computer use, multi-step coding, file/screenshot handling, text + images, and 100+ languages. Zuckerberg framed it as an early expression of "personal intelligence" — a model that lives on your device. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/meta-releases-muse-glimmer-ai-model-people-can-run-on-their-laptop), [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/metas-new-glimmer-ai-model-offers-a-hint-at-zuckerbergs-personal-intelligence-vision/)

Muse Spark (closed API) remains Meta's frontier offering; Glimmer is the Llama-equivalent for the Muse family — same open strategy, smaller capability footprint.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] OpenAI Expands Daybreak With GPT-5.6-Cyber, Its First Model to Hit "High" Cyber Threshold

**Background:** OpenAI's Daybreak program previously offered approved defenders access to standard frontier models (GPT-5.6 Sol) for everyday security work.

**New:** On August 10, OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber, a Sol-based variant fine-tuned specifically for vulnerability research and exploit-chain development, gated behind a new Daybreak Red tier (tighter vetting than Daybreak Blue). Internal eval: 95% completion rate on exploit-chain, authentication bypass, and privilege escalation tasks — versus 1.5% for standard Sol and 57.3% for its predecessor GPT-5.5-Cyber. OpenAI demonstrated the model by using it to find two previously unknown zero-days in V8 (Chrome's JS engine) that can be chained into a sandbox escape. Structure is now two tiers: Daybreak Blue (frontier models, approved defenders, everyday security work) and Daybreak Red (GPT-5.6-Cyber, tighter vetting, vuln research and exploit validation). [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/10/openai-gpt-astra-restrictions-safety-hacking-defenders), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-expands-daybreak-with-two-tiers-and-a-new-cybersecurity-model/), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-daybreak-gpt-5-6-cyber-ai-cybersecurity-breaches-126081100597_1.html)

**Why it matters:** A model that hits 95% on exploit-chain tasks is a qualitatively different tool than anything previously accessible — even to vetted defenders. This directly maps to your security concentration: Daybreak Red is how security researchers would get access.
