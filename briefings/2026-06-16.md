## Anthropic

### [UPDATE] Fable 5 / Mythos 5 export ban: White House meeting ends without resolution

**Background:** On June 12, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick ordered Anthropic to suspend global access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 after Amazon researchers jailbroke Mythos 5 and alerted senior White House officials. Anthropic complied and routed all users to Opus 4.8. The trigger was a China-linked group's suspected access to Mythos 5.

**New:** Anthropic executives flew to Washington and met with White House and Commerce Department officials on June 15. The meeting wrapped without a clear path forward — both sides remain publicly entrenched over whether Fable 5 poses a genuine national security risk. Anthropic maintains the government had already reviewed and approved Fable 5 before global release, and that only a narrow, non-universal jailbreak exists. The White House has not provided a restoration timeline. Separately, Anthropic told FedScoop that the ongoing ban puts federal contractor partnerships "in jeopardy" — contractors who have embedded Claude into government-facing systems now face contractual uncertainty even though the earlier February supply chain risk designation remains blocked by court injunction.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/15/anthropic-mythos-trump-ai.html), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4608906/anthropic-meeting-white-house-resolve-mythos-fable-ai-restrictions/), [FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/anthropic-says-trump-ban-puts-federal-contractor-partnerships-jeopardy/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/anthropic-set-to-meet-trump-officials-over-ai-security-concerns)

**Why it matters:** If you're building on Anthropic's API for any federal-adjacent use case, the contractor risk angle is now real and documented — not just theoretical.

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.178 — smarter permissions and nested skills

**Background:** v2.1.177 (June 12-13) was a 22-change stabilization focused on background sessions and the auto-fallback switch to Opus 4.8 after the Fable 5 suspension.

**New (v2.1.178, June 15):**
- `Tool(param:value)` syntax for permission rules lets you match on a tool's *input parameters*, not just the tool name — e.g., `Agent(model:opus)` blocks Opus subagents while allowing Sonnet; wildcards supported.
- Skills in nested `.claude/skills/` directories now load automatically when working in those directories; name collisions surface as `<dir>:<name>` so both remain accessible.
- Improved auto mode: subagent spawns are now evaluated by the safety classifier *before* launch, closing a gap where a subagent could request a blocked action without pre-review.

Sources: [Claude Code changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog), [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)

**Why it matters:** The `Tool(param:value)` permission rule is the most useful new primitive — you can now write `.claude/settings.json` rules that gate on *which model* a subagent tries to spawn, which matters for credit budget control under the new June 15 billing split.

**Background:** Altman (OpenAI), Amodei (Anthropic), and Hassabis (DeepMind) all confirmed attendance at the 52nd G7 in Évian-les-Bains, France (June 15-17) — first G7 with all three major AI lab CEOs present.

**New:** The summit opened June 15. The Élysée arranged a dedicated AI working lunch with G7 leaders and the three AI CEOs. The US delegation is blocking any multilateral governance commitments that could frame as binding constraints on American AI competitiveness. European members are pushing for stronger language; the expected communiqué is likely to be voluntary and watered-down compared to prior G7 AI statements. Key themes: youth safety, frontier risk in cyber and bio domains (context: same three labs co-signed the synthetic DNA letter to Congress days prior). No concrete outcomes from day 1.

## Practical tips

- **Claude Code permission rules just got more surgical.** The `Tool(param:value)` syntax in v2.1.178 lets `.claude/settings.json` block `Agent(model:claude-opus-4-8)` while allowing Sonnet subagents through — directly useful for controlling credit burn now that the billing split is live. Worth adding to your harness `settings.json` if you run multi-agent pipelines.
- **Fable 5 access is still down, Opus 4.8 is the ceiling.** With no restoration timeline from the White House, plan your tooling around Opus 4.8 as the top available model for the foreseeable future. The auto-fallback route already does this in Claude Code.
- **If you're using Claude in any federal-contractor-adjacent workflow**, track the FedScoop / NextGov coverage — the contractor jeopardy language from Anthropic is new and suggests the legal posture is shifting.
