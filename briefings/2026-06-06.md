## Anthropic

### [NEW] Making Claude a Chemist

Anthropic published the first entry in a new AI for Science chemistry series, benchmarking Opus 4.7 on NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance) spectroscopy interpretation — the most time-consuming routine step in synthetic chemistry, where analysts must manually match each spectral peak to an atom in a proposed structure. Opus 4.7 matched dedicated NMR software on most tasks and beat it on a subset of the more complex assignments. The team is expanding the AI for Science program explicitly to cover chemistry research, working with synthetic, computational, and analytical chemists to map where Claude saves time and where expert judgment is still required. No tool access or lab integration is described; this is pure spectral-data analysis.

**Why it matters:** Chemistry benchmarks have been lagging behind code and math in AI evaluations. This is the first first-party evidence that frontier models are crossing into specialist-grade analytical chemistry — relevant to drug discovery, materials science, and any domain where NMR is a bottleneck.

Sources: [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/making-claude-a-chemist), [Anthropic on X](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2062979607448682731)

### [NEW] Claude Partner Network: Services Track + Partner Hub

Three months after the Claude Partner Network opened applications, Anthropic formalized the Services Track (June 3) — a tiered structure for consulting and professional-services firms building and deploying with Claude in production. The program has attracted 40,000+ applicants and 10,000+ Claude-certified practitioners. Three tiers:

- **Select:** 10 certified practitioners, 2 production deployments, 1 public customer endorsement.
- **Preferred:** 100 certified practitioners, 15 active deployments, 3 published endorsements.
- **Global Premier:** 1,000 certified practitioners, 100 customers, coverage across 3+ regions, 15 endorsements, joint business plan with named executive sponsors.

A dedicated Partner Hub portal accompanies the launch for centralized application, training (via Skilljar), and co-marketing resources.

**Why it matters:** The tiered structure is a forcing function that should surface quality signal for enterprise buyers evaluating implementation partners. For Stack, the certification data is also a proxy for real-world Claude Code and Managed Agents adoption depth at the firm level.

### [UPDATE] Claude Security: Anthropic + Verizon DBIR — A Year of AI-Enabled Threat Data

**Background:** Claude Security entered public beta April 30, expanded Glasswing to 150+ orgs in June, and has coordinated disclosure on 2,100+ vulnerabilities.

**New:** Anthropic published findings from a year-long study (March 2025 – March 2026) of 832 accounts banned from Claude for malicious cyber activity, mapping their behavior to MITRE ATT&CK. Included in Verizon's 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report. Key findings:
- Attackers used Claude across 15 distinct ATT&CK techniques; some accounts used 40–50 techniques in sequence.
- Less than 2.5% of AI-assisted actions involved techniques outside MITRE's common baseline — attackers are using AI to automate and scale known playbooks, not discover novel exploits.
- AI has compressed the defender's response window on known CVEs from months to hours, making patching velocity the critical variable.
- Anthropic's threat-safeguard coverage is now mapped to the ATT&CK Navigator at red.anthropic.com.

Sources: [Anthropic / MITRE ATT&CK post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/AI-enabled-cyber-threats-mitre-attack), [LLM ATT&CK Navigator](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/attack-navigator/), [Verizon DBIR 2026 coverage](https://www.techtarget.com/searchsecurity/news/366643420/Verizon-DBIR-Key-takeaways-for-CISOs)

## Tools & Frameworks

### [UPDATE] Claude Code v2.1.164-167: CI/CD and Enterprise Reliability

**Background:** v2.1.163 (June 4) added `requiredMinimumVersion`/`requiredMaximumVersion` managed settings and `/plugin list`.

**New (v2.1.164-167, June 5-6):** This batch is almost entirely bug fixes on high-impact edge cases:

- **`claude -p` hanging:** Background shells that never exit now get stopped ~5 seconds after the final result once stdin closes — previously hung indefinitely.
- **Bedrock/Vertex/Foundry in CI:** `claude -p` was failing with "ANTHROPIC_API_KEY required" when `CI=true` and no Anthropic key was set, even when using platform auth. Fixed.
- **Bazel + EDR environments:** `$TMPDIR` was being overridden to `/tmp/claude-{uid}` for all commands instead of only sandboxed ones, breaking builds under strict sandboxing tools and endpoint detection/response agents.
- **Windows EEXIST crash:** Session-env directory creation failing with "file already exists" on read-only attribute paths or OneDrive locations.
- **Org-managed permission rules:** Permissions from managed settings were not persisting for the entire session. Fixed.
- **Background session task persistence:** Background sessions in `claude agents` were losing their running tasks when reattached after a Claude Code update.
- **UX:** Startup notices now grouped by severity; session info and announcements share a single line per launch. Launch-prompt warnings stay pinned below the input rather than scrolling away on long startup output.

Sources: [Claude Code changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog), [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases), [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)
